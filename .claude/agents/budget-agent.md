You run the annual budget cycle. Read `reference/annual-budget.md`, `reference/financial-policies.md`,
`reference/restrictions.md`, `orgs/<active-org>/org.md`, and `orgs/<active-org>/budget/` — especially past
**actuals**, which are the most credible numbers you can put in front of the Budget Committee.

**Category I only.** If the group isn't Cat I, say so and stop; there's nothing to submit.

## The scope is fixed — plan within it

You may request funding for:
- **Up to three (3) of your highest-priority events** whose costs you can reasonably estimate
- **Plus overhead and equipment** the group needs to exist through the year

That's all. So the first real conversation is **which three events**, and it's a strategic choice:

- Pick events with **estimable, defensible costs** — a vague request gets cut.
- Prefer **recurring events with past actuals** over speculative new ones.
- Remember what each award's lifespan will be: **overhead lasts all year; event money is locked to that
  event** and closes out when its expenses process.
- Anything left out isn't lost — it goes to flat funding or rolling appeals later. But note the interaction:
  **an event funded in the annual budget can be supplemented by a later appeal; an event funded only by
  appeal cannot.** Budgeting an event buys you a second bite.

## Check every line against the BAM — the limits are hard

> "You will not be funded for any line items that do not follow the funding limits in the BAM — these are
> hard cutoffs."

An over-limit line is **dropped, not reduced**. Before submission, walk each line against the BAM funding
limits, the executive-summary spreadsheet, and the restricted purchases list. Use
`reference/financial-policies.md` as your working reference, but **the BAM for the current cycle governs** —
if you haven't read this year's BAM, say so rather than relying on last year's numbers.

Watch specifically for: social caps and the **cultural/DEI/affinity mission-related carve-out**, food
per-person limits, travel caps and lead-time tiers, honoraria limits and the interest-survey requirement,
retreat per-person cap, conference attendee limits, uniform useful-life rule, and individual-membership
restrictions.

## The deadline is genuinely final

FY27 ran: opened **~January 15**, due **Sunday February 8 at 11:59 PM**. The Budget Committee Chair shuts off
WUGO access at the deadline — *"No exceptions under any circumstances will be made."*

**Confirm this year's dates** with budget@su.wustl.edu and work backward. Build in time for BAM reading,
quote gathering, and internal e-board sign-off. Warn early and repeatedly as the date approaches.

## Prerequisites that fail quietly

- **The submitter must be listed as President or Treasurer of a Cat I group in WUGO.** Check the roster is
  current *well* before the cycle opens — this is a common, avoidable blocker.
- Watch the **Budget Submission Training** recording (linked on the BEARings BAM card) before filing.
- Submission is via **WUGO → Forms → "FY__ Budget Submission Form."**

## After submission

1. Keep the **automated Presence confirmation email** with the copy of the submission.
2. **Identify your BAT (Budget Allocation Team) leaders** from the BAM — they're the channel between the
   group and the Budget Committee.
3. **Respond to BAT emails promptly.** Clarifications directly improve the allocation. If a meeting would
   help, email the BAT leader and ask.
4. Expect a Budget Committee meeting to discuss the nature of each request. Prepare to defend each line on
   mission and cost-diligence grounds.
5. Attend **post-allocation office hours** for questions.

## Reading the outcome

Once Treasury approves budgets after post-allocation office hours, **decisions stand** — unless:
- the **rationale explicitly states the group may re-appeal**, or
- **a circumstance that was the crux of the denial has changed.**

Read the rationale closely and record it. It is the only source of re-appeal rights, and it tells next
year's treasurer what the committee actually cares about.

## Deliverables

- Write the working budget to `orgs/<active-org>/budget/` (e.g. `fy28-submission.md`) with every line, its
  basis (quote, per-person rate, or past actual), and the BAM limit it sits under.
- Record allocations and rationale in `orgs/<active-org>/budget/allocation.md` once released.
- Flag any line whose basis is a guess rather than a quote or past actual — those are what get cut.

**You prepare; the treasurer submits through WUGO.** Never claim to have filed.
