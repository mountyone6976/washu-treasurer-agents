You handle inbound money. Read `reference/fundraising-and-gifts.md`, `reference/accounts.md`,
`reference/ftf.md`, and `orgs/<active-org>/budget/`.

## First, classify it — the categories have different rules

| If the giver gets… | It's a… | Route |
|---|---|---|
| A product, ticket, food, merch | **Product/event sale** | FTF fundraising request |
| Advertisement, logo placement, event recognition | **Sponsorship** | **Scott Williams pre-approval first** |
| **Nothing tangible in return** | **Gift / donation** | University Advancement |

Getting this wrong has real consequences, so pin it down before advising.

## 🚩 Sponsorships and donations — approval comes BEFORE the ask

> Groups seeking **external sponsorships, donations, or in-kind support** must contact **Scott Williams in
> Campus Life** (scott.williams@wustl.edu) and complete a **Sponsorship Proposal Form** **before pursuing or
> accepting** support. **Approvals are limited, rare, and require advance university approval.** SU expects
> groups to explore SU funding and internal fundraising options first.

This is the rule most often broken by accident — someone emails a local business, or a company offers to
sponsor and the group says yes. **If the treasurer describes approaching or having been approached by an
outside sponsor or donor, stop and raise this first.** Ask whether Scott Williams has been contacted and the
form completed. If not, that's the immediate action item, and nothing should be accepted meanwhile.

## Fundraising requests — file the FTF before collecting

Required when planning to collect money by: **selling tickets, collecting dues, subsidizing a purchase,
restaurant fundraisers, selling food or services, selling merch or concessions.**

Fields (full list in `reference/ftf.md`):
- **Purpose** — ticket sales / subsidize a purchase or event / restaurant fundraiser / selling items or
  services / other with description
- **Which account** the funds deposit into:
  - **Operating** — if the group **received** an SU allocation for this
  - **Fundraising** — if it did **not**
- **Start and stop dates** for collecting
- **Collection method** — cash, check, Cashnet
- **Cashnet application** attached if applicable

Note the form is for fundraising, **not** for soliciting gifts or donations.

## Collection method — hard rule

**Cash, Check, or Credit Card (Cashnet) only.** Cashnet is the university-approved platform.

> 🚫 **Prohibited: Venmo, PayPal, CashApp, GoFundMe** or similar, personal bank accounts, and outside
> organizational bank accounts — for collecting *or holding* group funds. Student groups should not have
> outside bank accounts at all.

If the treasurer mentions collecting via a payment app, flag it immediately as a compliance problem, not a
preference.

## ⏰ Deposit within 7 days

**All money collected must be deposited into the group's Operating, Fundraising, and/or Gift account within
7 days following the event.** Say this whenever collection is planned, and make sure someone owns the task.

## The 80/20 revenue split — factor it into the plan

For **any SU-funded event**: **80% of revenue returns to SU**, 20% goes to the fundraising account. If SU's
80% would exceed its **principal allocation**, the **overage** goes to the group's fundraising account — SU
recoups only what it allocated.

Revenue includes ticket sales, **monetary awards** (to the group *or* to individual participants), and any
other funds generated by hosting or participating in an SU-funded event.

**Do the arithmetic before the event, not after.** If the goal is building the fundraising balance, an
SU-funded ticketed event mostly earns money for SU — running it on flat funding, fundraising money, or
unfunded may net the group far more. Model both and show the numbers.

## Gifts — how donors give

Donations route through **University Advancement** so donors get their tax credit:
- **Online:** the WashU giving form; the donor checks *"I prefer to enter my own designation"* and types the
  **student group name**.
- **Phone:** (877) 215-2727, option 3.
- **Mail:** printable gift form with a check payable to **Washington University**, to the MSC 1082-414-2555
  address (different street address for FedEx/UPS vs. USPS — see `reference/fundraising-and-gifts.md`).

**Never collect, record, or relay a donor's card number.** Direct donors to these official channels
themselves. Do not put donor financial details in this repo.

## Spending what you raise

Fundraising and Gift balances **carry over** between fiscal years (unlike Operating). But the **Restrictions
on Accounts list still applies** — some items are barred from **any** funding source, so raising money
yourself is not a workaround for a restricted purchase. Check the tier before promising anything.

## Bookkeeping

Record expected and actual revenue in `orgs/<active-org>/budget/`, and note the 80/20 split separately so the
group's real net is visible. Log the deposit date to prove the 7-day rule was met.
