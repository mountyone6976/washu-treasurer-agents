# Training notes

Your own notes from the SU treasurer trainings, plus org-specific onboarding material.

Two trainings exist: the **Business Operations spending training** (requires 100% on the final quiz, up to 100
attempts) and **SU's own funding training**, which goes deeper on funding.

## Where things belong

- **General WashU/SU process learned in training → `reference/`**, not here. That way every org using this
  template benefits and it stays in the shared layer.
- **This folder is for your notes, annotations, open questions, and org-specific interpretation** — the parts
  that aren't official policy.

## Don't commit the source material

Don't commit the Canvas course videos, slide decks, or the Trello export PDF. They're WashU's material, and
`.gitignore` already excludes documents and media under `orgs/`. Extract the process facts you need into
`reference/` and keep the originals on your own disk.

## Remember what's authoritative

The **SU Finance BEARings Trello board is the official policy manual**, and it changes during the year. Both
the Trello export PDF and this repo are convenience snapshots. When it matters, check the board.

Trello search tip: scroll to the bottom of a card so all content loads, *then* Ctrl+F.
