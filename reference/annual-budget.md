# The annual budgeting cycle

**Category I groups only.** Submitted during **spring semester** for the **following** academic (fiscal)
year.

## What you request

- Funding for **up to three (3) of your highest-priority events** that you can reasonably estimate costs for
- Plus any **overhead and equipment** expenses the group needs to exist throughout the year

That's the whole scope. Everything else during the year comes from flat funding, rolling appeals, or funds
you raise.

## The documents

| Resource | What it is |
|---|---|
| **BAM — Budget Allocation Manual** | The authoritative guide to the process, funding limits, and changes to SU's financial structure. Read it fully. |
| **Executive summary spreadsheet** | A condensed view of the funding limits |
| **Restricted purchases list** | What won't be funded — check before writing lines |
| **Budget Submission Training** recording | Linked on the BEARings Budget Allocation Manual card |

> ⚠️ **"You will not be funded for any line items that do not follow the funding limits in the BAM — these
> are hard cutoffs."** An over-limit line isn't reduced, it's dropped. Check every line against the BAM
> before submitting.

## How to submit

- Submitted through **WUGO** (Presence). On WUGO, go to **Forms** and find the **"FY__ Budget Submission
  Form."**
- You must be listed as a **President or Treasurer of a Category I group in WUGO** to participate — so
  **keep your WUGO roster current** well before the cycle opens.
- After submitting you get an **automated email from Presence** with a copy of your submission. Keep it.

## The deadline is genuinely hard

The FY27 cycle ran: opened **January 15**, budgets due **Sunday, February 8 at 11:59 PM**.

> The Budget Committee Chair's own words: *"This is a hard deadline. I will be shutting off access to WUGO
> when the deadline hits, and you will no longer be able to edit or submit them after this date has passed.
> No exceptions under any circumstances will be made."*

Expect a similar mid-January open and early-February due date each year, and **confirm the current dates**
with budget@su.wustl.edu. Work well in advance — access disappears at the deadline.

## After you submit

1. Your **Budget Allocation Team (BAT)** leaders — the coordinators between your group and the Budget
   Committee — email you with any questions. **Identify your BAT assignment from the BAM (page four in the
   FY27 edition).**
2. **Respond promptly.** Clarifications directly help the committee allocate your budget properly. If you
   think a meeting would help, email your BAT leader and ask for one.
3. Budget Committee members meet with each group to understand the nature of each request.
4. The committee convenes through the spring to review budgets and make allocation decisions.
5. Allocations are completed and released.
6. The Budget Committee holds **post-allocation office hours** for questions.

## Finality and re-appeal

> Once Treasury approves the budgets after post-allocation office hours, **the Budget Committee's decisions
> stand** — unless:
> - the committee's **rationale explicitly says the group may re-appeal**, or
> - **a circumstance that was the crux of the denial has changed.**

Read the rationale carefully; it's the only place your re-appeal rights are granted. Note this interacts
with the rolling-appeal rule: supplementing an event **is** allowed if that event was last funded in your
**annual budget**.

## When the money arrives

Allocations deposit **automatically** into your Operating account, with split lifespans:

- **Overhead** funds — available all school year, or until spent
- **Event** funds — locked to that specific event; **close out once the event's expenses process**

Operating funds are **use-it-or-lose-it** and reset at fiscal year end. See [accounts.md](accounts.md).

## Practical prep

The best predictor of a good allocation is **defensible cost estimates**. Keep actuals from this year's
events in `orgs/<slug>/budget/` — a real past cost is the most credible number you can put in front of the
committee, and it's what next year's you will wish you had written down.
