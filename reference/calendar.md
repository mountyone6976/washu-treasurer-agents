## Rolling deadlines — the ones that bite

| Clock | Deadline |
|---|---|
| **Money collected at an event** | Deposit within **7 days of the event taking place** |
| **Money collected outside an event** (dues, merch sales) | Deposit within **7 days of collecting the money** |
| **Personal purchase to be reimbursed** | Workday expense report within **7 days of the purchase date** |
| **An item removed from your expense report** by Business Ops | Resubmit it on a **brand-new expense report within 7 days of removal**, or it stops being eligible |
| **Supplier invoice** (vendors who don't take cards) | Submit the FTF within **7 days of whichever comes first**: the service being completed, the items being received, or the invoice landing in your inbox |
| **WashU credit card purchase** | FTF **at least one day before** you plan to buy — **same-day card purchases are not allowed** |
| **Event registration in WUGO** | At least **14 days in advance** — and "event" includes GBMs, exec meetings, tryouts, rehearsals, practices, meets and games. See [campus-life.md](campus-life.md) |
| **Package delivered** | Pick up at **DUC Suite 270** reception within **7 days of receipt** |
| **Appeal under $1,000** | Submit **≥ 3 weeks** before the funds are needed |
| **Appeal of $1,000 or more** | Submit **≥ 4 weeks** before the funds are needed |
| **Travel appeal** | File **4+ weeks** before the event to get the higher caps — see [travel.md](travel.md) |
| **Speaker over $10,000** | Interest survey open **≥ 5 days**, before the appeal — see [financial-policies.md](financial-policies.md) |

> **The 7-day expense-report rule has exactly one escape hatch.** Late submissions need a special exception
> request **approved by the Business Manager**, and each student group gets **one approved exception per
> academic year** — the board calls it a "one-time lifeline." Spend it once and the next late report is
> simply not reimbursed.

## Weekly rhythm

- **Daily Report** — account balances refresh **every weekday (Mon–Fri)**. Check before any spending
  request, and remember it **overstates** what you have (see [trello-board.md](trello-board.md)).
- **Credit card hours** — **Thursday and Friday, 10 am–12 pm and 2 pm–4 pm**, Campus Life office,
  **DUC Suite 270**. Only two days a week, so a purchase that misses Friday afternoon waits until Thursday.
  **Hours do not run all year:** fall hours start **Sept 3**, spring hours start **Jan 21** and end
  **Apr 23**, there are **no hours over spring break (Mar 14–21)**, and Thanksgiving week moves to
  **Mon/Tue Nov 23–24**.
- **Office hours** (deposits, drop-offs, questions) — **Mon–Fri 8:30 am–5:00 pm**, DUC Suite 270. Closed on
  the university holidays listed below.

## Monthly — the "Sent Back" expense-report clock

If Business Ops sends an expense report back to you, there is a **hard monthly deadline to fix it**, and a
day later it is **canceled and ineligible for reimbursement**. This is the single most-missed set of dates
on the card.

| Reports sent back in | Fixed by | Canceled / ineligible |
|---|---|---|
| September 2026 | **Oct 7** | **Oct 8** |
| October 2026 | **Nov 8** | **Nov 9** |
| November 2026 | **Dec 7** | **Dec 8** |
| December 2026 | **Dec 16** | **Dec 17** |
| January 2027 | **Feb 7** | **Feb 8** |
| February 2027 | **Mar 7** | **Mar 8** |
| March 2027 | **Apr 7** | **Apr 8** |
| April 2027 | **May 9** | **May 10** |
| May 2027 | **Jun 7** | **Jun 8** |

> The card's exact phrasing is, e.g., **'September "Sent Back" Expense Reports fixed by: Oct 7'** and
> **'…canceled and ineligible for reimbursement: Oct 8'** — it doesn't spell out whether "September" means
> *sent back during September* or *September's reports*. Either reading lands on the same action date, so
> use the dates as written. Note the **December row is Dec 16/17, inside December itself** (it tracks the
> end-of-term blackout, not a following month), and the card lists **no August 2026 or June/July 2027
> row** — don't assume the pattern continues; ask your Business Coordinator.

## Fiscal year end

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Before FY end | **Spend down your Operating account** — it resets to $0 and does not roll over |
| Before FY end | Unused funds from **completed events** are swept back into SU's general pool **twice a year**, at the end of the fall and spring semesters — a finished event's leftovers are not yours to redeploy later |
| Before FY end | If you have room left, contact Business Operations to **pivot funds to approved activity** rather than wasting them. "Use it or lose it" is not licence to buy random items |
| Before FY end | Final reimbursements and supplier payments — respect the 7-day clocks, the **May 4–12** blackout and the **Jun 1 – Jul 15** blackout |
| Spring | **Re-register the group in WUGO** — requires 10 active members, a president and a treasurer, and at least one registered *and approved* event during **Aug 29, 2026 – May 3, 2027**. See [campus-life.md](campus-life.md) |
| Transition | **Handoff to the next treasurer**; update the WUGO roster (treasurer + president required, and they can't be the same person) |

Fundraising and Gift balances carry over — only Operating resets. See [accounts.md](accounts.md).

## Any time

- **Rolling appeals** — Category I only, as needed, subject to the semester cutoffs above (**Nov 1 / Nov 15**
  in the fall, **Apr 4 / Apr 11** in the spring). File early; travel caps depend on lead time.
- **Reallocations** — when spending needs change from the approved allocation. Approval must land **before**
  the money moves.
- **Canceled event retrievals** — file promptly so funds can be reused for appeals.
- **Fundraising requests** — FTF **before** you start collecting.
- **Sponsorship approval** — via Scott Williams in Campus Life, **before** approaching a sponsor.
- **New fiscal-year financial policies** — released once approved by Treasury, in the **second week of the
  fall semester**. The caps in [financial-policies.md](financial-policies.md) are the prior year's until
  then; confirm anything material with finance@su.wustl.edu.
