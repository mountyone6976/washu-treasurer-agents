# The treasurer year — deadlines and rhythms

> **Always confirm exact dates on the BEARings "Important Financial Dates" card.** It's the authoritative
> calendar and it changes. Check it periodically — especially near the end of each semester.

## Rolling deadlines — the ones that bite

| Clock | Deadline |
|---|---|
| **Money collected** at an event | Deposit within **7 days following the event** |
| **Personal purchase** to be reimbursed | Workday expense report within **7 days of the purchase date** |
| **Supplier goods received / service provided** | Submit the FTF within **7 days** |
| **Event registration in WUGO** | At least **14 days before** the event |
| **Package delivered** | Pick up at DUC 270 within **7 days** |
| **Travel appeal** | File **4+ weeks** before the event to get the higher caps |
| **Speaker over $10,000** | Interest survey open **≥ 5 days**, before the appeal |

## Weekly rhythm

- **Daily Report** — account balances and activity update **every weekday (Mon–Fri)**. Check before any
  spending request.
- **Credit card hours** — **Thursday and Friday**, 10 am–12 pm and 2 pm–4 pm, DUC 270. Only two days a week,
  so a purchase that misses Friday afternoon waits until Thursday.
- **Monthly** — there is a **last day to fix an expense report** each month. On the Important Financial Dates
  card.

## Fall semester

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Over the summer | SU determines **flat funding** allocations |
| Before school | Complete **treasurer training** (Business Operations spending training; 100% required to pass) |
| Early fall | **SU's own funding training** — deeper than the Business Ops one |
| **Second week of school** | **New fiscal-year financial policies** released, once approved by Treasury. Re-read them; the caps in `financial-policies.md` are last year's until then |
| First week of fall | Treasury communications begin — minutes, P&T emails, reports posted |
| **September 30** | Deadline for **Organization Registration / SU Recognition Application** (fall only; new recognition and Cat III status take effect the following semester) |
| Fall semester | **Category change applications** (III→II, II→I) |
| End of semester | **Spending blackout periods** — check the dates and plan around them |

## Spring semester

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **~January 15** | Annual budgeting cycle **opens**; BAM released |
| Before the deadline | **Budget Submission Training** (recording on the BEARings board) |
| **~Early February** (FY27: Sunday Feb 8, 11:59 PM) | **Budgets due — hard deadline, WUGO access shuts off, no exceptions** |
| Through spring | BAT leader questions; Budget Committee meetings and deliberation |
| Spring | Allocations released, then **post-allocation office hours** |
| Spring | **Name change applications** reviewed (spring only; effective next fall) |
| Spring | **Category change applications** (this process runs each semester) |
| Spring | Second travel window: eligibility for 500+ mile trips depends on whether you travelled 500+ miles in the fall |
| End of semester | **Spending blackout periods** |

## Fiscal year end

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Before FY end | **Spend down your Operating account** — it resets to $0 and does not roll over |
| Before FY end | If you have room left, contact Business Operations to **pivot funds to approved activity** rather than wasting them |
| Before FY end | Final reimbursements and supplier payments processed — respect the 7-day clocks and blackout dates |
| Transition | **Handoff to the next treasurer**; update the WUGO roster (treasurer + president required) |

Fundraising and Gift balances carry over — only Operating resets.

## Any time

- **Rolling appeals** — as needed, Category I only. File early; travel caps depend on lead time.
- **Reallocations** — when spending needs change significantly from the approved allocation.
- **Canceled event retrievals** — file promptly so funds can be reused for appeals.
- **Fundraising requests** — before you start collecting.
- **Sponsorship approval** — via Scott Williams, **before** approaching a sponsor.
