# Campus Life — the non-money approvals

Campus Life controls **whether your event may happen**. SU controls **whether SU money pays for it**. Two
separate gates; you need both.

## WUGO event registration

- **All events must be registered and approved in WUGO** for the event to take place — this is Campus Life
  policy, not an SU finance rule.
- **This includes minor events**: practices, GBMs, and rehearsals.
- **Register at least 14 days before the event.**

### The change you need to understand

**Business Operations no longer requires proof of WUGO registration before spending.** The FTF no longer
asks about it. You **will be allowed to spend before an event is approved.**

> ⚠️ **But the risk transferred to your group.** If funds are spent on an event that is **later denied and
> not permitted to occur, the student group is responsible for repaying those funds.** And until the
> repayment is made, **the group may not make any additional purchases or spend any additional funds.**
> **No exceptions.**

So: the *permission* to spend early is real, and the *penalty* for guessing wrong shuts down your entire
group's spending. Register in WUGO first anyway. Treat the relaxed rule as convenience for timing, not as
license to spend on unapproved events.

## What Campus Life policy covers

- Risk management
- Catering and food safety rules
- Campus space reservations
- Alcohol policies
- Guest speaker clearances
- Student group good-standing requirements

**Your responsibility:** actively review and understand these policies **before** holding the event and
**before making any financial commitments.**

## Your Campus Life Advisor

Your advisor enforces and guides you through these policies. They are the right person for questions about
whether and how an event can happen.

- **Be proactive** — schedule a meeting **early in your planning phase**, not once you're committed.
- **Keep them fully updated.** Share event changes and updates **even after the event has been officially
  approved.**
- **Contracts:** work with your advisor to get supplier/performer contracts signed. Route contracts through
  them rather than signing yourself.

> SU's stated golden rule: following Campus Life and university policies is the best way to get your
> registered events approved without delay.

## Roster

Keep your **WUGO roster updated**, including both a **treasurer and a president** — required to stay
compliant with SU. The roster also matters operationally: travel FTFs ask you to upload the trip roster and
to name any **coach, graduate students, or individuals not on the WUGO roster** who are travelling.
