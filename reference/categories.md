# Categories I, II, III

An SU-recognized group's **category** determines what funding it can get. Know your category before
reading anything else about money — half the policies below only apply to Category I.

| | **Category I** | **Category II** | **Category III** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic SU resources (free) | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Flat funding** | ✅ $15/active member | ✅ $15/active member | ❌ none |
| **Annual budget submission** | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| **Rolling appeals** | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Typical profile | Highest-spending groups | Groups that don't need a large budget; money arrives with little effort | Entry point for newly recognized groups |

- **Category III** is where all newly SU-recognized groups start. After one semester they may apply to
  upgrade to Category II.
- **Only Category I groups may submit financial appeals during the year**, and only Category I groups
  submit annual budgets. If you're Cat II or III, flat funding + fundraising + gifts is your whole toolkit.
- Basic resources are provided free of charge to Cat I, II, and III alike (see the On-Campus Resources
  list on the SU website).

## Changing category

Upgrades go III → II → I, one step, via the **Category Change Application** on WUGO (Forms tab). Runs
**each semester**; new status takes effect at the start of the following semester.

Process:
1. Submit the application on WUGO.
2. Activities Committee Chair emails to schedule a meeting.
3. Your group gives a **5-minute presentation**, then ~5 minutes of Q&A with the Activities Committee
   Team (ACT).
4. ACT recommends to the Activities Committee; AC discusses and votes.
5. You're emailed the decision **and the rationale**.

Questions: activities@su.wustl.edu.

## Related processes (same office)

- **New SU recognition** — Organization Registration Form or SU Recognition Application on WUGO. Must
  first meet with Scott Williams (scott.williams@wustl.edu). **Fall semester only.** New recognition and
  Cat III status take effect the following semester.
- **Name change** — "SU Group Name Change Application" on WUGO. **Reviewed in spring only**, effective
  the following fall.
- **Deregistration** — email activities@su.wustl.edu. SU also periodically contacts apparently inactive
  groups.

> Compliance note: being a compliant student group requires a **minimum of 10 active members** — this is
> also the floor used for the minimum flat funding calculation.
