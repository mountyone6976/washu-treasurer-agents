# Categories I, II, III

An SU-recognized group's **category** determines what funding it can get. Know your category before
reading anything else about money — half the policies in this folder only apply to Category I.

> **Two different things are called "category" at WashU.** SU's **funding categories** are I / II / III
> (this file). Campus Life's **student group categories** are *interest areas* — Academic/Pre-Professional,
> Cultural, Diversity & Inclusion, Environmental & Sustainability, Governing, Health & Wellness, Honorary,
> Media, Performing Arts, Political Action, Recreation, Religious & Spiritual, Service and Community
> Engagement, Social Justice, Sorority & Fraternity, Sports Clubs. Your interest area determines your
> **Campus Life Advisor and Business Coordinator**, not your funding. The `Category` column in the board's
> `Campus_Life_Advisors_&_Business_Coordinators…xlsx` / `FY27_Campus_Life_Advisors…xlsx` spreadsheets is
> the **interest area** (`Title | Category | Campus Life Advisor | SU Business Coordinator`) — do **not**
> read your funding tier off it.

**Finding your funding tier.** No open card or attachment on the board publishes a per-group I/II/III list.
Your tier is recorded in `orgs/<slug>/org.md`; confirm it with activities@su.wustl.edu (the Activities
Committee Chair owns categories) or your Business Coordinator. The **Student Activities Fee Mastersheet
2026-27** card links a live sheet of every allocation made to every group, which is the best check on what
your group actually received — but it is on SharePoint and requires WashU SSO, so an agent cannot read it
for you.

## What each category gets

| | **Category I** | **Category II** | **Category III** |
|---|---|---|---|
| General SU resources (free) | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Flat funding** | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ *"Do not have access to: … Flat Funding"* |
| **Annual budget submission** | ✅ | ❌ *"Do not have access to: Budgeting"* | ❌ |
| **Rolling appeals** | ✅ | ❌ *"Do not have access to: … Appeals"* | ❌ |
| Fundraising / gift accounts | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Typical profile | *"typically spend the most money"* | *"a good place for groups to stay that do not spend a large budget since this money is granted with little action needed"* | *"the entry point for all newly recognized SU groups"* |

- **Category I** is the only tier with all three SU funding streams: flat funding **and** an annual budget
  **and** rolling appeals ([funding-sources.md](funding-sources.md)).
- **Category II** gets the Category III resource set **plus flat funding** — and nothing else. The Cat II
  card carries an explicit *"Do not have access to:"* checklist listing **Budgeting** and **Appeals**.
- **Category III** gets *"General SU Resources"* only. Its *"Do not have access to:"* checklist lists
  **Budgets**, **Appeals**, and **Flat Funding**. All newly SU-recognized groups start here, and the
  Campus Life handbook agrees: *"Newly recognized student groups will initially be placed in funding
  Category III under Student Union."*
- **After one semester in Category III**, a group may submit an upgrade application to become Category II.
- Basic resources are free to Cat I, II, and III alike — full list in [su-resources.md](su-resources.md).

> **Only Category I may submit annual budgets or rolling appeals — verified, no exceptions found.** The
> live **Rolling Appeals Process** card opens with *"Only Category I SU-recognized student groups may
> submit financial appeals throughout the school year,"* the **Annual Budgeting Process** card restricts
> the spring cycle to Category I groups, and the Cat II and Cat III cards both list Budgeting and Appeals
> under *"Do not have access to."* **Category II has no appeal rights of any kind** on the current board.
> If you are Cat II or III, flat funding (Cat II only) + fundraising + gifts is your whole toolkit.

> **One unexplained route.** The contacts card lists *"Executive Appeals"* among the VP of Finance's areas,
> and the dates card has a *"Last Day to Submit an Appeal to VP of Finance <$1000 and to the SU Exec
> Council"* line, but **no card states who is eligible** for an Exec Council appeal. Don't assume it opens
> appeals to Cat II/III — confirm with finance@su.wustl.edu. See [appeals.md](appeals.md).

## Membership floor and the WUGO roster

**Sourced from the 2026-2027 Student Group Handbook** (Campus Life, attached to the board's *Campus
Life/University Policies* card) — the SU cards are silent on membership counts.

- A student group is *"any group of **ten (10) or more** active undergraduate WashU students"* recognized
  by SU, Campus Life, or another department.
- **Must maintain a minimum of 10 members at all times.**
- **Must have at least a president and a treasurer listed as officers** — and at recognition they
  **cannot be the same person**.
- **Must have an up-to-date roster in WUGO at all times** with active membership. This is the requirement
  behind several money gates: all Presidents and Treasurers **listed on the WUGO roster** must complete the
  Business Operations Spending Training, and only someone listed as President or Treasurer of a Cat I group
  **in WUGO** can participate in the budgeting cycle ([annual-budget.md](annual-budget.md)).
- Membership is limited to **enrolled WashU undergraduate or graduate students**; **graduate students may
  not serve as an executive or officer** of an undergraduate group.
- *"Failure to maintain membership requirements may result in student group conduct referral."*

**Spring re-registration** (every SU- and Campus Life-recognized group, via WUGO) requires all of:

1. **10 active members** on the WUGO roster.
2. At least **one president and one treasurer** listed as officers on the WUGO roster.
3. At least **one registered and approved** program, event, or activity during the current academic year
   (**August 29, 2026 – May 3, 2027** for this year).

> **Missing re-registration is a category reset, not a warning.** *"Failure to re-register before the
> spring deadline will result in deactivation of the group for the next academic year. Deactivated groups
> may reapply as a new student group (at the entry level) by the specified deadlines in the fall
> semester."* Entry level means **Category III** — a Cat I group that skips re-registration loses budget
> and appeal rights entirely. The handbook does not print the spring re-registration date; get it from
> your Campus Life Advisor.

## Compliance — what a pause suspends, and how to lift it

Falling out of compliance does not cost you your category, but it freezes the money. From the identical
compliance checklists on the **RULES: Operating / Fundraising / Gift (In-Depth)** cards:

> *"If a group falls out of compliance with these rules, your financial privileges will be temporarily
> paused until the issue is resolved."*

| Suspended during a compliance pause |
|---|
| 💳 **WashU credit card usage** (purchasing cards) |
| 💸 **Reimbursement processing** |
| 🤝 **Supplier payments** |
| 📥 **Deposit processing** |
| 🔄 **Reallocations** |

**How you get it back:** *"As soon as the compliance issue is fixed, we will happily unfreeze your account
and get your financial services right back up and running!"* SU reaches out first — *"we'll reach out to
help get things back on track, but unresolved issues will be referred to Campus Life for further review."*
Separately, being out of compliance *"can result in the denial of your purchase, payment, or reimbursement
requests."*

Two compliance items are worth checking today:

- **Business Operations Spending Training (Canvas).** *"All Presidents and Treasurers listed on the student
  group WUGO roster must complete this training before any financial activity can take place."* A **100%
  score is required**; below 100% you retake it until you reach it.
- **The out-of-compliance list.** The HEADS UP list carries a dated spreadsheet of groups out of compliance
  on the training (`Groups_that_are_out_of_compliance_…_09.01.26.xlsx`). Check whether your group is on the
  current one before you plan any spending. See [trello-board.md](trello-board.md) for how to pull it.

## Changing category

Upgrades go **III → II → I**, one step at a time, via the **Category Change Application** on
**WUGO → Forms**. Decided by the **Activities Committee**; run by the **Activities Committee Chair**
(activities@su.wustl.edu — see [contacts-su.md](contacts-su.md)).

**Timing.** *"New Category status will take effect at the start of the following semester. This process
occurs semesterly."* The **Important Financial Dates** card gives one dated deadline:

| Cycle | Deadline on the board |
|---|---|
| **Fall 2026** | *"Category Change and New Group Applications Due:* **Sept 25 by 11:59 pm**" |
| **Spring 2027** | *"SU Category Upgrade and Name Change Requests Due"* — **listed with no date** |

> **The Category Change card contradicts itself on timing.** Its opening sentence says *"you will be able
> to submit an application during the fall semester,"* while its first bullet says *"This process occurs
> semesterly"* — and the dates card lists an upgrade deadline in **both** semesters. Assume semesterly, but
> confirm the spring date with activities@su.wustl.edu, since the board doesn't print it.

Process:

1. Submit the **Category Change Application** on WUGO (Forms tab).
2. The Activities Committee Chair emails you to schedule a meeting.
3. Your group gives a **5-minute presentation**.
4. The **Activities Committee Team (ACT)** runs **~5 minutes of Q&A**.
5. ACT makes a **recommendation** to the Activities Committee, which discusses it.
6. The Activities Committee **votes**, and you're emailed the decision **and the rationale**.

**Criteria — the card says the group must meet all seven:**

- Summary of group mission
- Summary of past events/programming
- **How and why your group would benefit from an upgrade — use of SU resources**
- **Breakdown of past and proposed spending**
- Demonstrated audience
- Demonstrated sustainability
- Distinctiveness

> **The treasurer owns two of those seven.** "Breakdown of past and proposed spending" and "use of SU
> resources" are ledger work, not narrative. Pull past actuals from `orgs/<slug>/budget/ledger.csv` and the
> prior-year `FY__` daily reports on the board ([trello-board.md](trello-board.md)), and build the proposed
> side against the caps in [financial-policies.md](financial-policies.md) so the numbers you present are
> ones SU could actually fund.

## New SU recognition

- **Prerequisite meeting.** *"Before completing the respective form, prospective groups are required to
  meet with the Assistant Director for Student Groups and Student Involvement, Scott Williams
  (scott.williams@wustl.edu)."*
- **Which form on WUGO → Forms:** the **Organization Registration Form** for a brand-new group; groups
  *"already registered through Campus Life, but … seeking SU Recognition"* file the **SU Recognition
  Application** instead.
- **Fall only.** *"This process will now only occur in the fall semester."* New SU recognition **and
  Category III status take effect at the start of the following semester.**
- **Review path:** AC Chair **and Campus Life** review the application and may email questions → groups
  passing the Campus Life review and a secondary committee review **meet with members of the Activities
  Committee** → those members **present the group** to the committee → committee questions, discusses, and
  **votes** → the **AC Chair emails the decision and the rationale**.
- **Criteria — must meet all five:** Distinctiveness, Sustainability, Programming, Audience, SU Resources.
- Campus Life's own guide: <https://students.wustl.edu/starting-new-student-group/>. Per the handbook the
  process *"typically takes 60-90 days,"* and recognition also requires a **formalized constitution**, a
  **WashU faculty/staff advisor** who signs an advisor agreement, and **10 active undergraduate members
  including a president and a treasurer (not the same person)**.
- **Campus Life recognition is a separate, lower status**, for *"groups who are not yet eligible for
  Student Union recognition"*; it can be held *"for up to two academic semesters"* while the group builds
  toward SU recognition. A Campus Life-recognized group has no SU category, no SU accounts, and no SU
  funding.

> ⚠️ **Stale deadline on the recognition card.** It still reads *"the deadline to fill out either
> 'Organization Registration' or 'SU Recognition Application' is **September 30th, 2024**."* The current
> **Important Financial Dates** card says new group applications are due **September 25, 11:59 pm** for
> Fall 2026. Use the dates card and confirm with activities@su.wustl.edu.

## Name change

- File the **"SU Group Name Change Application"** on WUGO. The **Activities Committee Chair reviews** it.
- **Spring only:** *"These applications will only be reviewed and approved in the spring semester and will
  take effect at the beginning of the next fall semester."* (The same card's earlier sentence says the
  change *"will go into effect the following semester"* — the spring-only sentence is the specific one.)
- You'll be emailed the status once a decision is made. The card invites a follow-up to
  activities@su.wustl.edu *"if you feel your application has gone unaddressed for an extended period."*
- **Treasurer follow-through the board doesn't cover.** Your project numbers, daily-report rows, and FTF
  event names are all keyed to your group's name. The board says nothing about who updates those after a
  rename — ask your **Business Coordinator** to confirm the Workday project name changed before you file
  your next FTF ([ftf.md](ftf.md)).
- **Mission changes** are listed as an Activities Committee Chair topic in [contacts-su.md](contacts-su.md)
  but have **no card on the board**. Route them to activities@su.wustl.edu.

## Deregistration and deactivation

Two different ways a group can stop being recognized:

1. **Voluntary deregistration** — *"Reach out to the Activities Committee Chair at activities@su.wustl.edu
   if you wish to voluntarily deregister your group."* No form is specified. **The treasurer sends that
   email** — nobody can do it on the group's behalf.
2. **Inactivity** — *"Periodically, SU will reach out to groups that appear to be inactive to determine if
   deregistration is necessary."* No cadence, criteria, or appeal route is published; the fastest defence
   is the re-registration evidence above (a registered, approved event on the record and a current WUGO
   roster).

Campus Life adds a third: **deactivation** for missing spring re-registration, and **loss of registration
status** as a possible sanction if a group is found to have violated the Student Group Code of Conduct.

> **The board is silent on what happens to your money.** Nothing on any card says what becomes of
> Operating, Fundraising, or Gift balances when a group deregisters or is deactivated. Operating resets to
> $0 at fiscal year end regardless, while Fundraising and Gift carry over
> ([accounts.md](accounts.md)) — so a deregistration mid-year has real balances at stake. Ask
> finance@su.wustl.edu and the SU Business Manager **before** initiating anything voluntary.

## Reading these cards yourself

```bash
python tools/su_board.py cards --list "STUDENT GROUP ORGANIZATION"
python tools/su_board.py card "Category Change Process"
```

Seven cards, all in the open **STUDENT GROUP ORGANIZATION: Process** list: SU Student Group Recognition
Process, Category Change Process, Name Change Process, Deregistration Process, Category I Groups,
Category II Groups, Category III Groups. **Read the checklists** — the entitlement lists and every approval
criterion above live in checklists, not descriptions ([trello-board.md](trello-board.md), trap 2).

`2026-2027_Student_Group_Handbook.pdf` attachment for the membership and re-registration rules, which no
SU card covers.

> title names a *different* prior-year chair — and that card sits in an **archived** list. Get the current
