# Financial policies — funding caps

> ⚠️ **These are the FY26 policies.** Per FY27 VPF Cora Johnson: *"These are the financial policies from
> FY26. New financial policies are being written for the upcoming year, and will be released when they are
> approved by Treasury during the second week of school. Use these guidelines as a reference."*
>
> Use them to plan and to sanity-check requests, but **verify before relying on a specific number**, and
> replace this file once FY27 policies are published. Questions: finance@su.wustl.edu.

**Scope: these policies apply to Category I groups that budget and appeal for events.**

Caps are **hard cutoffs** — a line item over the limit isn't trimmed, it's denied.

## Socials

| Rule | Limit |
|---|---|
| Total cost per social event | **≤ $5,000** |
| Per-person cost | **≤ $30/person** |
| Food | **≤ $20/person** |
| Frequency | **One (1) social event per fiscal year** |
| Attendee mix | **≥ 60% of attendees must be group members** |
| Location | Must occur in a **single location** |

**Venue funding by attendance:**

| Attendees | Venue funding |
|---|---|
| < 140 | **$0** |
| 140–199 | $2,500 |
| 200–299 | $4,000 |
| 300+ | $5,000 |

**Exceptions:** venue-cap exceptions are granted by Treasury **case by case for cultural, religious, or
safety reasons**; a group granted one moves to the **next tier cap**.

**Important carve-out:** group-wide social and formal events for **cultural, diversity & inclusion, and
affinity groups may be considered mission-related**, and therefore **do not count toward the social cap**.
If your group is one of these, argue mission-relatedness rather than accepting the social cap by default.

**GBMs are never funded via appeals** — use flat funding.

## Honoraria (speakers and guests)

| Rule | Limit |
|---|---|
| Total honoraria per group per year | **≤ $25,000** |
| Speakers over $10,000 | **No more than one (1)** |
| For any speaker over $10,000 | Must run an **interest survey open ≥ 5 days** |

The survey's design and administration are up to you, but you must be prepared to **report results
demonstrating interest and expected attendance**. Run it early — it's a precondition, not a formality.

## Travel

**The Travel Cap: $682 per active travelling member** — set equal to the Student Activities Fee (SAF).

**Lead time changes your caps. Filing 4+ weeks out is worth real money:**

| Line item | Appeal filed **< 4 weeks** out | Appeal filed **4+ weeks** out |
|---|---|---|
| Hotels | $150 / room / night | **$200** / room / night |
| Flights | $300 / round trip | **$400** / round trip |
| Sedans | $60 / day | **$70** / day |
| SUVs | $70 / day | **$80** / day |
| Vans | $80 / day | **$90** / day |
| Gas | $0.20 / mile | $0.20 / mile |

**Distance rules:**
- Travel **under 500 miles** is funded **as if the group is driving**.
- **Driving is not permitted for travel 500+ miles.**
- **Over 500 miles:** if the group did **not** travel over 500 miles in the fall, it's eligible for **two**
  such trips in the spring. If it **did** travel 500+ miles in the fall, it may submit **one** appeal for
  500+ mile travel in the spring.

## Retreats

- **≤ $100 per person.**
- If the retreat is also a travel event, the **travel cap still applies on top**.

## Conferences

| Rule | Limit |
|---|---|
| Attendees at a **travel** conference | **≤ 4** |
| Attendees at a **local** conference | No restriction |
| **Annual cap on group travel spend** | **$3,000** |

Definitions: a **travel conference** has event category "Travel (50–500 miles)" or "Travel (500+ miles)";
a **local conference** has category "Local Off-Campus."

Counting against the conference travel cap: items classified **Lodging**, **Registration/Membership Fee**,
and **Transportation**.

## Individual memberships

SU funds **only** membership costs required to keep the **organization** in compliance with its governing
body. SU will **not** fund membership costs associated with any **single student** — even if required to
participate in a particular event.

This does not change how **registration fees for specific tournaments** are funded. For individual
organizational membership fees, use **dues, fundraising, gifts, or the SU Opportunity Fund** (if eligible).

## Uniforms

Funded only if **both** are true:
1. Required to participate in an event, **and**
2. Have a **useful life of more than one year**.

## Revenue from SU-funded events

- **80% of revenue returns to SU**; **20%** goes to your fundraising account.
- If SU's 80% would exceed its principal allocation, the **overage goes to your fundraising account** —
  SU recoups only what it allocated.
- Revenue includes but isn't limited to: ticket sales or similar, **monetary awards** (to the group *or* to
  individual participants), and any other funds generated by hosting or participating in an SU-funded event.

## How SU thinks about these limits

Straight from the Financial Policies card:

- These caps describe **limits on what SU will allocate** toward certain items within a fiscal year. Groups
  are free to **supplement SU's funding** with their own fundraising, flat funding, or gifted funds beyond
  what SU provides.
- They apply **only to Category I groups**, the only ones that budget and appeal for events.
- **Anything not explicitly listed above is not subject to any specific funding limit** — it's judged on the
  funding principles instead.
- > **"SU's thought process during budgeting or appeals is to fund events UP from $0 rather than starting
  > with the requested amount and finding ways down from there."**

That last point should shape how you write requests: every line has to earn its place from zero. A padded
total isn't trimmed proportionally — unjustified lines simply never get added.

## Paying students for services

**$0 this fiscal year.** Per FY26 policy: *"This year, we cannot pay students for services due to issues with
payroll through Workday. Students that are not student employees may be paid."*

Combined with the all-accounts prohibition on **payments to WashU employees, including student employees**,
the practical rule is: don't plan on paying a student for a service. Confirm the current-year position with
finance@su.wustl.edu before promising anyone anything.

## Other standing rules

- **Re-appeals:** refused, unless the event was last funded in the group's annual budget.
- **Deficit spending:** SU will not fund items already spent or committed.
- **Restrictions on Accounts:** maintained by the VPF with the SU Business Manager; see
  [restrictions.md](restrictions.md).
- **Flat funding:** see [funding-sources.md](funding-sources.md).
