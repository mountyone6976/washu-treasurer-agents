# Where the money comes from

Three SU funding streams, plus what you raise yourself. All SU awards deposit **automatically** into your
Operating account.

| Stream | Who's eligible | When | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Annual budget allocation** | Cat I | Submitted each spring for the *following* fiscal year | Your 3 highest-priority events + overhead/equipment |
| **Flat funding** | Cat I & II | Automatic, annual | Almost anything not on the restricted list |
| **Rolling appeals** | Cat I | As needed, throughout the year | Specific events/needs not in your budget |
| **Fundraising / gifts** | All | Any time | See fundraising-and-gifts.md |

## Flat funding — your flexible money

The most useful money you have, because it's the least restricted.

**Rate:** $15 per active member.

| Category | Allocation | Cap |
|---|---|---|
| Cat I | $15 × active members, capped at **150% of your previous year's spending** | **$1,350** max |
| Cat II | $15 × active members, capped at **150% of your previous year's spending** | **$1,350** max |
| Cat III | None | — |

**Minimum: $150.** That's $15 × 10 members, 10 being the minimum for a compliant group. The 150%-of-prior-
spending cap cannot push you below this floor — a group that spent $0 or $1 last year still gets $150,
not $0 or $1.50.

**What flat funding can be used for:** any event not on the restricted allocations list — explicitly
including **social events, general body meetings (GBMs), and information sessions**, none of which SU
funds through appeals. It can also **top up** an event that was budgeted or appealed for but not funded to
the level you wanted. Its whole purpose is flexibility.

> Cat III note: the BEARings board describes Cat III as receiving no flat funding in the category
> comparison, but the Flat Funding card describes Cat III receiving $15 × the 10-member minimum. These
> conflict. If you're Cat III, confirm with finance@su.wustl.edu rather than assuming either.

## Annual budget allocation

Submitted in spring for the next fiscal year; covers your **three largest events plus overhead**. Full
process in [annual-budget.md](annual-budget.md).

Note the split lifespan once it lands: **overhead** money lasts all year, **event** money is locked to
that event and closes out when the event's expenses process.

## Rolling appeals

For needs that arise during the year. **Category I only.** Routing depends on size — under $1K goes to the
VP of Finance, over $1K to the Speaker of the Treasury. Full process, including the lead-time caps that
make filing early worth real money, in [appeals.md](appeals.md).

## What none of these will do

- **Fund deficit spending.** SU does not fund anything you have already spent or committed funds toward.
  Secure the money first.
- **Fund GBMs via appeals.** Use flat funding.
- **Supplement a prior award via re-appeal**, unless the event was last funded in your annual budget.
