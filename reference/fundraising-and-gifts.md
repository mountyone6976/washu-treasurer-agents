# Fundraising, sponsorships, and gifts

## Fundraising vs. gift — get this distinction right

| | **Fundraising** | **Gift / donation** |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | You sell something, or a sponsor gets something back | A **voluntary transfer of money with no product, service, or tangible benefit expected in return** |
| Routed through | You collect it (Cash / Check / Cashnet) | **University Advancement** |
| Form | **FTF — Fundraising request** | No FTF; donor uses Advancement channels |
| Lands in | Fundraising account | Gift account |
| Rollover | Carries over | Carries over |

If the giver receives advertisement, logo placement, or event recognition, it is a **sponsorship** — that's
fundraising, not a gift.

## Fundraising

Two types:

1. **Product & event sales** — merchandise (t-shirts), event tickets, food, restaurant fundraiser nights.
2. **Sponsorships** — money from a person or company in exchange for a **tangible benefit** (advertisement,
   logo placement, event recognition).

### File an FTF fundraising request first

Required when you plan to collect money by: selling tickets, collecting dues, subsidizing a purchase,
restaurant fundraisers, selling food or services, or selling merch and concessions.

The form asks: the purpose, **which account** the funds land in, your **start and stop** collection dates,
and your **collection method**. Attach a **Cashnet application** if applicable. Full field list in
[ftf.md](ftf.md).

**Which account do collected funds go into?**
- **Operating** — if you **received** an SU allocation for this
- **Fundraising** — if you did **not** receive an allocation

### Collecting money — approved methods only

**Cash, Check, or Credit Card (Cashnet).** These are the only university-approved payment methods for
student organizations. **Cashnet is the university-approved platform** for collecting funds.

> 🚫 **Prohibited:** personal bank accounts, outside organizational bank accounts, and third-party payment
> platforms — **Venmo, GoFundMe, PayPal, CashApp**, or similar — to collect or hold organization funds.
> Student groups should not have outside bank accounts at all.

### ⏰ Deposit deadline

**All money collected must be deposited into your SU Operating, Fundraising, and/or Gift account within
7 days following the event.**

### The revenue split — plan for it

For **any SU-funded event**, **80% of revenue generated returns to SU**; **20%** goes to your fundraising
account.

- If SU's 80% would exceed its **principal allocation**, the **overage** goes to your fundraising account —
  SU recoups only what it actually allocated.
- **Revenue** includes but is not limited to: ticket sales or similar processes, **monetary awards** (to the
  group as a whole *or* to individual participants), and any other funds generated by hosting or
  participating in an SU-funded event.

Practical consequence: a ticketed, SU-funded event mostly earns money for SU, not you. If building your
fundraising balance is the goal, that changes which events are worth running with SU funds.

## Sponsorships and gifts — approval comes first

> Groups seeking **external sponsorships, donations, or in-kind support** must contact **Scott Williams in
> Campus Life** (scott.williams@wustl.edu) and complete a **Sponsorship Proposal Form** **before pursuing
> or accepting** support.
>
> **Sponsorship approvals are limited, rare, and require advance university approval.** Groups should first
> explore Student Union funding and other internal fundraising options.

Do not solicit a company before this is cleared, and do not accept something already offered before
checking. This is the rule most easily broken by accident.

## How donors give a gift

All donations process through **University Advancement** so donors receive their official tax credits.

**Online:** the WashU giving form. The donor checks **"I prefer to enter my own designation (specify
below)"** and types **your student group name** so funds route to your organization.

**By phone:** toll-free **(877) 215-2727**, choose **option 3**. Visa, MasterCard, Discover, and American
Express accepted. The donor provides their own card number, expiration, and name — **you should never
collect or relay card details on a donor's behalf.**

**By mail:** the donor prints and completes the printable gift form and includes a credit card payment or
check payable to **Washington University**.

## Spending from Fundraising and Gift accounts

Same three methods as Operating — credit card, Workday reimbursement, supplier payment (see
[spending-methods.md](spending-methods.md)).

**The SU "Restrictions on Accounts" list still applies.** Some items can't be bought with operating funds;
a further set can't be bought with **any** funding, fundraising and gift included. Raising the money
yourself does not unlock restricted purchases.

## If the group disbands

Leftover Fundraising and Gift money stays on campus — it transfers to a general **Office of Student
Success** account, and the **Vice Chancellor for Student Success** reallocates it to other student programs,
campus events, and active clubs.
