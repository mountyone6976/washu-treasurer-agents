# Funding principles and restrictions

> **Failure to comply results in denial of purchase approvals and reimbursements**, and can escalate to a
> **compliance pause** on all your group's financial privileges. Check this *before* committing — a
> restricted purchase found at reimbursement time leaves whoever paid out of pocket unpaid.

Source: the **Restrictions on Accounts** card. It can be amended mid-year — re-read it before each
spending push, and confirm against the live board (`python tools/su_board.py card "Restrictions"`).

## 🚫 Prohibited in the OPERATING account

Operating = budget allocations, flat funding, and appeals.

- Advertising / publicity expenses not provided by Student Union
- Costs pertaining to **non-WashU or non-undergraduate** students
- Donations
- **Fines, damages, late or cancellation fees**
- Fundraising efforts
- **Giveaways** — anything given away as a thank-you or promotion, **including graduation cords, stoles,
  and apparel**
- **Membership dues or purchases pertaining to an individual** (as opposed to the group)
- **Networking events** — events/items related to job or internship recruiting
- Personal items
- **Resources SU already provides free** — e.g. plastic or non-compostable dinnerware, which is available
  through the Student Sustainability Board (see [su-resources.md](su-resources.md))
- Travel insurance

## 🚫 Prohibited in ALL accounts — Operating, Fundraising, AND Gift

Raising the money yourself does **not** unlock these:

- **Alcohol and drinkware**
- Dangerous / harmful items
- Donation payment reimbursements
- **Gifts, gift cards, and prizes — when using the WashU credit card.** These must be paid for through a
  **reimbursement** instead. (Note the shape of this rule: the item isn't banned, the *payment method* is.)
- Purchases using **Meal Points / Bear Bucks**
- **Security deposits**
- **Service provider reimbursements** — see the services rule below
- **Payments to WashU employees — including student employees**

## The services rule — a common, expensive mistake

Hiring outside professionals (DJs, photographers, graphic designers, guest speakers):

- **All payments must go directly from the university to the supplier** — WashU credit card, ACH, or
  university check.
- **Students may not pay for professional services out of pocket and seek reimbursement.** The accounting
  system cannot reimburse personal payments for services.
- **Why it matters:** services require contracts, tax documents, and liability coverage. Paying
  out-of-pocket can make **you personally liable** if something goes wrong.
- **You also can't hire a WashU employee or student employee as an outside supplier** — the payroll rule
  blocks it. Nuance from FY26 policy: **students who are not student employees may be paid**, but paying
  students for services was set at **$0 this year** due to Workday payroll issues. Confirm current-year
  status before promising anyone anything.

## SU funding principles — how requests are judged

- **Need vs. want.** Prioritize need.
- **Mission-related events over socials.**
- **Line items essential to the mission of the event.** SU's example: food to entice attendance at a GBM is
  unnecessary; supplies for a mission-relevant activity are necessary.
- **Fiscal responsibility.** Expect: Are there local alternatives? Is it a league-sanctioned tournament?
  Has the group done due diligence to minimize costs?

> **SU builds funding UP from $0** — it does not start from your requested number and cut down. Every line
> must earn its place on its own. Padding doesn't survive, and unjustified lines don't get partial credit.

> **Anything not explicitly capped has no specific funding limit.** The caps in
> [financial-policies.md](financial-policies.md) are a finite list; items outside it are judged on the
> principles above rather than against a number.

## Structural rules that function as restrictions

| Rule | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Approval before spending** | Appeals and reallocations must be **fully approved before you spend a dollar**. Buy early or sign a contract early and the new funds **cannot** cover it — the group *or you personally* owes the bill |
| **Spending before funding** | Never reimbursed. **No exceptions** |
| **Deficit spending** | SU will not fund what you've already spent or committed |
| **No retroactive appeals** | SU does not approve appeals for money already spent |
| **Operating cannot go negative** | The account may not fall below $0 |
| **Re-appeals** | Refused, unless the event was last funded in your **annual budget** |
| **GBMs** | Not funded via appeals — use flat funding |
| **Revenue** | 80% of revenue from an SU-funded event returns to SU |
| **Budget hard cutoffs** | Line items over the BAM limits are not funded at all |
| **Advisor-run transactions** | Advisors, departments, and campus partners may not purchase for you or transact from your accounts |
| **Outside accounts** | No outside bank accounts, personal accounts, or Venmo/PayPal/CashApp/GoFundMe |
| **Event later denied in WUGO** | Repay the funds; **no further purchases until repaid.** No exceptions |

## Compliance pause — what "out of compliance" actually costs

If your group falls out of compliance, Business Operations reaches out to help, but **unresolved issues are
referred to Campus Life** — and your financial privileges are **paused** until fixed. Suspended during a
pause:

- 💳 WashU credit card usage
- 💸 Reimbursement processing
- 🤝 Supplier payments
- 📥 Deposit processing
- 🔄 Reallocations

That is your entire financial toolkit. Privileges resume once the issue is resolved.

**Financial integrity:** misuse of funds, fraudulent activity, or intentional mismanagement is referred
**immediately** to Campus Life and University Administration.

## Conflict of interest

Students conducting group business must act **ethically and objectively**, complying with applicable laws,
regulations, and University policy including the **University Judicial Code**.

**Disclose before spending:** purchasing goods or services from a business in which a **student, family
member, or friend has a financial interest** — or may directly benefit — is a potential conflict of
interest, and **must be disclosed to the SU Business Manager for review before funds are disbursed.**

**Consequences of a breach:** referral to the **University Judicial Administrator**, possible legal action,
**freezing of the student group account**, and termination of the supplier relationship.

**Gifts from businesses:**
- **Cash or monetary gifts of any kind or amount may never be accepted by students.**
- Nominal promotional items (pens, notepads, trinkets) may be acceptable, but **never in return for a
  business favor**, and never where they could influence business judgment or create an appearance of
  impropriety.
- **Any promotional benefit from a business transaction belongs to the student group**, not an individual
  student.
- **No member may accept any sum from a supplier** "rewarding" them for the decision to do business.
- Give **equal opportunity** to firms wishing to pursue business with the University.
- **Review all offers of gifts with the SU Business Manager before accepting.**

## Practical pre-flight

1. Not on either prohibited list above — and check **which list**, since Fundraising/Gift money doesn't
   escape the all-accounts list.
2. Within the relevant cap in [financial-policies.md](financial-policies.md) — caps are hard.
3. Funding **already approved** (not deficit spending, not pre-approval spending).
4. Correct account and budget name identified.
5. It's a purchase, not a service being paid out of pocket.
6. Event registered in WUGO ≥14 days out.
7. No conflict of interest — and if there might be, disclose to the Business Manager first.
8. Genuinely unsure? Ask your Business Coordinator **before** buying. The VP of Finance rules on ambiguity.
