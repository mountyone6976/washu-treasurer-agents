# Spending methods

Three ways to spend, from any account (Operating, Fundraising, or Gift). Pick the method first — it
determines which form you file and what documentation you need.

| Method | Use when | Form | Key deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| **WashU Credit Card** | Buying goods/services online, by phone, or locally | **FTF** | Receipt to coordinator after purchase |
| **Personal Reimbursement** | Someone paid out of pocket | **Workday Expense Report** (no FTF) | **7 days from purchase date** |
| **Supplier Payment** | Vendor can't take a card; needs check or ACH | **FTF** | FTF within **7 days** of goods received / service provided |

Not sure which fits? Ask your Business Coordinator.

---

## 1. WashU Credit Card

**Two flavors on the FTF:** *non-travel* and *travel*.

- **Non-travel:** goods and services purchased **online or over the phone in the SU office**. Physical
  cards can be checked out on a **very limited basis** for local shopping.
- **Travel:** airfare, ground transportation, lodging, and registration.

**Process:**
1. Check your balance on the Daily Report (see [accounts.md](accounts.md)).
2. Check the rules for what you're buying ([restrictions.md](restrictions.md),
   [financial-policies.md](financial-policies.md)).
3. **File the FTF in advance** ([ftf.md](ftf.md)).
4. Come to **DUC 270 during credit card hours** — Thu & Fri, 10 am–12 pm and 2 pm–4 pm.
   - Only treasurers should come in. If you can't, name a substitute in the FTF **purchaser** field.
5. Your coordinator gives you a **credit card sheet** with the card details, the **shipping address**, and
   **their receipts email address**.
6. **Ship everything to the 875 Kingsland address** — it's on the credit card sheet.
7. **Send the receipt to your Business Coordinator's receipts email address.** Each coordinator has a
   *different* receipts address; it's on the credit card sheet.

**Packages:** watch the BEARings package card / SharePoint sheet for delivery status. Pick up at the SU
Campus Life Office, **DUC Suite 270** — any staff member can retrieve it. **Do not search the package area
yourself**; staff track and document all pickups. Collect within **7 days** of receipt.

## 2. Personal Reimbursement — Workday Expense Report

**The one transaction that does NOT use an FTF.** Reimbursements go through Workday instead.

> ⏰ **The expense report must be submitted within 7 days of the purchase date.**

### 🎟️ The "One-Time Lifeline" — you get one per year

Late submissions require a **special exception approved by the Business Manager**, and **each student group
is allowed only ONE approved exception warning per academic year.** Treat it as the group's single "oops"
card. The same one-per-year limit covers reviving a canceled "Sent Back" report (below).

Track whether yours has been used — record it in `orgs/<slug>/org.md`, because a successor treasurer
otherwise has no way to know it's already spent.

### 📥 "Sent Back" reports expire

If your Business Coordinator returns a report in Workday (usually for a receipt problem), it's marked
**Sent Back**. **Resubmit immediately.**

> Any report left in "Sent Back" status past the deadlines on the **Important Financial Dates** card is
> **automatically canceled by the system and is no longer eligible for reimbursement.**

Reviving a canceled report needs a Business Manager exception — and that's the same once-a-year lifeline.
**Check your Workday inbox weekly** so a Sent Back report doesn't quietly expire.

Separately: if the team removes a single line item for a bad receipt, that item must be **resubmitted on a
brand-new expense report within 7 days of its removal.**

### The student-employee requirement

Access to the **Workday expense report module requires being a student employee.**

- If the **treasurer is a student employee**: easiest path — make the purchase and submit the report yourself.
- If the **treasurer is not a student employee**: not a problem. **Any group member who is a student
  employee can submit the report, even if they did not pay** for the expense.
  - The person who paid gives the student employee a **proper receipt** promptly, to stay inside the 7-day
    window.
  - The **treasurer's job** is to point that student employee at the Workday training resources on the
    BEARings board and to supply the **budget information to enter plus the group's project number**.
  - Once the student employee is paid into their bank account, **they pay back the person who actually
    paid** for the expense.

### Before you ask your coordinator for help

Open the **"Reimbursements in Workday"** card on the BEARings board and review the **three attached
resources** — step-by-step instructions for submitting and tracking a reimbursement.

### Set up Workday notifications — do this now

**Business Coordinators do not email you when an expense report is sent back.** Anyone in your group who
enters expense reports must enable Workday email notifications so a bounced report reaches their WashU
account. Instructions are attached to the "Process: Spending from your Operating Account" card.

### Filling out the report — the mechanics that route it correctly

- **One event per expense report**, maximum **25 lines**. More than that, start a second report.
- **Each unique receipt gets its own line.** Don't bundle receipts onto one line.
- **Expense Item: "Social and Entertainment - Purchases and Supplies"** — this is what routes the paperwork
  to the right place.
- **Use the comment field to state the business purpose** — how the purchase serves the event or group.
  Accounting needs that context to approve.
- **Upload PDF, JPEG, or PNG only. HEIC files cannot be opened** by the accounting system — a common
  problem with iPhone photos, so convert before uploading.
- Official instructions: `workday.wustl.edu/items/create-expense-report/` → "Create an Expense Report for
  Yourself (as a worker)". Three walkthrough PDFs are attached to the BEARings "Reimbursements in Workday"
  card.
- The **Expenses Hub** in Workday is the dashboard: create reports, see pending items, track history.
- **Keep the physical receipt** until the report reads as fully processed — you'll want it if an upload is
  blurry.

### What will get you rejected

- **A bad receipt is the single most common cause of reimbursement delay.** See receipt requirements below.
- Expenses incurred **before funding was available** — never reimbursed, no exceptions.
- **Services paid out of pocket** — DJs, photographers, designers, speakers must be paid directly by the
  university. See [restrictions.md](restrictions.md).
- **Rental cars paid personally** — must go on a WashU credit card. See [travel.md](travel.md).
- **Monthly bank/credit card statements or canceled checks by themselves** are not acceptable proof of
  purchase.

## 3. Supplier Payment — check or ACH

For a supplier providing goods/services that **cannot accept the WashU credit card**.

**Order matters here:**

1. **Work with your Campus Life Advisor to get a contract signed first.** Students generally do not sign on
   the university's behalf — route it through your advisor.
2. Send the supplier a **blank W-9 and ACH form** (both are on the BEARings board) and **request an invoice**.
3. **Check the signatures.** W-9s and ACH forms must carry **actual signatures or DocuSign signatures** —
   **typed cursive fonts are not accepted.** This is a routine rejection cause.
4. Once you have **all** documents, **submit the FTF within 7 days** of the goods being received or the
   service being provided.

**Attachments required:** invoice, W-9, ACH electronic payment form, plus anything else that helps.

**You may not contract WashU employees or student employees for services.** Student group accounts can't
process payroll or hourly wages, so there's no way to pay them through your standard methods.

## Receipt requirements — every receipt, every method

A valid receipt must show **all** of:

| Requirement | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Store / vendor / website / organization** | Where it was purchased from |
| **Date of purchase** | |
| **Items purchased** | For restaurants or caterers, the **food and drink items** must be itemized |
| **Amount** | |
| **Proof it was actually paid** | A line showing the **last 4 digits of the card used**, or a **$0.00 balance due** / "Paid". A bank statement screenshot works only *alongside* an otherwise-complete receipt |

A card slip with a total and no itemization is not a valid receipt. Neither is an order confirmation, and
neither is a monthly statement or canceled check on its own — those routinely omit the itemization.

**Best practice:** photograph the physical receipt or download the official PDF invoice **immediately**
after purchase. Reconstructing one later is how the 7-day window gets missed.

**If a receipt is missing details, hold off uploading it** until you have the required info — while keeping
the 7-day clock in mind. Submitting a bad receipt costs more days than fixing it does.

## Hard spending rules

- **Spending before funding:** SU will not reimburse students or groups for expenses incurred **before
  funding is available** to the organization. **No exceptions.**
- **Use university accounts for everything:** all expenditures must be paid from approved SU Operating,
  Fundraising, or Gift accounts. Group funds must **never** be held in personal accounts or apps (Venmo,
  PayPal, CashApp, etc.).
- **No outside bank accounts** for the organization.
- **Restricted purchases** can mean denial of purchase, denial of reimbursement, and **possible
  disciplinary action** — see [restrictions.md](restrictions.md).
- **Watch for spending blackout periods** and the **monthly last-day-to-fix-an-expense-report** dates, both
  posted on the Important Financial Dates card. Check near semester end.
