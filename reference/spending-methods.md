# Spending methods

Three ways to spend, from any account (Operating, Fundraising, or Gift). Pick the method first — it
determines which form you file and what documentation you need.

| Method | Use when | Form | Key deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| **WashU Credit Card** | Buying goods/services online, in the Campus Life office | **FTF** | FTF **at least 1 day** before you buy; receipt to your coordinator **before you leave the office** |
| **Personal Reimbursement** | Someone paid out of pocket | **Workday Expense Report** (no FTF) | **7 days from purchase date** |
| **Supplier Payment** | Vendor can't take a card; needs check or ACH | **FTF** | FTF within **7 days** of whichever comes first: service completed, items received, or invoice received |

Not sure which fits? Ask your Business Coordinator.

## 1. WashU Credit Card

There is no card in your pocket: you come to the **Campus Life office** and make the purchase there, on
your **Business Coordinator's** card. That in-person purchase is the only card route the current board
describes.

**Two flavors on the FTF:** *non-travel* and *travel*. One form covers both — see [ftf.md](ftf.md).

> ⚠️ **Removed claim — checking out a physical card.** An earlier version of this file said physical cards
> could be checked out "on a very limited basis" for local shopping. The current board says nothing of the
> kind, and the **archived** prior-year card on the subject says the opposite: "**Local credit cards will no
> longer be available**," with local purchases handled by out-of-pocket-plus-reimbursement instead. If your
> group holds its own pro card, that arrangement isn't documented on the board — ask your Business
> Coordinator.

> ⏰ **No same-day card purchases.** The FTF must be in **at least one day** before you plan to buy.
> But you **do not** have to wait for a formal approval email from your Business Coordinator — once the
> form is submitted you can come in during credit card hours.

**Hours and place:** **Thursdays and Fridays, 10:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m. and 2:00 p.m.–4:00 p.m.**, at the
Campus Life office, **DUC Suite 270**. Hours are marked *subject to change*, and the **Important Financial
Dates** card carries the start dates each semester, holiday closures, **special one-off card hours** (e.g.
around Thanksgiving), and **spending blackout periods** — check it before you plan a trip in. See
[calendar.md](calendar.md).

**Process:**

1. Check your balance on the Daily Report (see [accounts.md](accounts.md)).
2. Check the rules for what you're buying ([restrictions.md](restrictions.md),
   [financial-policies.md](financial-policies.md)).
3. **File the FTF at least one day in advance** ([ftf.md](ftf.md)).
4. Come to **DUC Suite 270** during credit card hours. **Scan the QR code at the desk to sign in** — your
   name appears on the waitlist screen and your Business Coordinator calls you up when it's your turn.
5. Make the purchase:
   - **Amazon, Target, Walmart** — your coordinator logs you into the SU **business account**. You build
     the cart, then they help you check out and place the order.
   - **All other online purchases** — you complete the transaction yourself, in the office.
   - **Travel** — book through one of the coordinator's travel accounts (below).
6. **Wrap-up before you leave.** Your coordinator **closes the payment screens** to protect the card data,
   and **reviews your itemized receipt on the spot** against university accounting standards.
7. **Email the receipt to your coordinator's receipts inbox, and confirm they got it before you leave.**

**Receipts inboxes** — each coordinator has their own. Which one is yours depends on your assigned Business
Coordinator; see [contacts-su.md](contacts-su.md).

### Travel on the card

**Available booking accounts:** United, American, Delta, Southwest, **Expedia**, **Booking.com** (airlines
and hotels) and **Enterprise** (ground travel).

- **Rental cars must go on the WashU credit card** — never a personal reimbursement, because that's what
  carries university insurance. Your coordinator gives you the **Enterprise corporate account and billing
  number**; you book **one vehicle at a time**; and you collect the **mandatory Enterprise insurance card**
  from the office before the trip.
- **Airbnb and VRBO can *only* be paid out of pocket and reimbursed** afterward through a Workday expense
  report. They cannot go on the card.
- More in [travel.md](travel.md).

### Contracted suppliers: pay in full

The preferred practice is a **single full payment** rather than splitting a deposit and a final balance —
it keeps the budget clean and avoids administrative delay. If your vendor demands a deposit, talk to your
**Campus Life Advisor and Business Coordinator ahead of time**.

### Accidental personal charges

If a personal item ends up on the WashU card (a personal item mixed into a group order, a personal account
linked at checkout), **the student who received the item must reimburse the university for the exact cost.**
Double-check the cart and make sure no personal account is linked before you check out.

## 2. Personal Reimbursement — Workday Expense Report

**The one transaction that does NOT use an FTF.** Reimbursements go through Workday instead.

> ⏰ **The expense report must be submitted within 7 days of the purchase date.**

### 🎟️ The "One-Time Lifeline" — one per academic year

Late submissions require a **special exception request approved by the Business Manager**, and **each
student group gets only ONE approved exception warning per academic year**. The Reimbursements Quick
Reference Guide is explicit that the single exception covers **both** late submissions **and** reviving a
canceled "Sent Back" report — it is one lifeline, not one of each.

Track the offer or request, approval, and use separately in `orgs/<slug>/org.md`. Record which report the exception covers. A filing exception does not change the funding or receipt requirements.

### 📥 "Sent Back" reports expire

If your Business Coordinator returns a report in Workday (usually for a receipt problem), it's marked
**Sent Back**. Open the existing report from **My Tasks**, read the reviewer comments, and correct and resubmit it by the coordinator's stated deadline. If the coordinator combined reports, verify the surviving report ID, receipt lines, and original statuses in Workday before editing. Keep each purchase on its existing funding allocation in the ledger and avoid counting the consolidated claim twice.

> Any report left in "Sent Back" status past the deadlines on the **Important Financial Dates** card is
> **automatically canceled by the system and is no longer eligible for reimbursement.**

Those deadlines run **monthly**: each month's Sent Back reports must be fixed by roughly the **7th of the
following month**, and are **canceled the next day**. The exact dates for each month are on the Important
Financial Dates card — see [calendar.md](calendar.md). Reviving a canceled report needs a Business Manager
exception, and that's the same once-a-year lifeline. **Check your Workday inbox weekly** so a Sent Back
report doesn't quietly expire.

Separately: if the team removes a **single line item** for a bad receipt, they leave a comment explaining
what's needed, and that item must be **resubmitted on a brand-new expense report within 7 days of its
removal.**

### The student-employee requirement

**Workday only allows active student employees to log in and submit expense reports directly.**

- If the **treasurer is a student employee**: easiest path — make the purchase and submit the report
  yourself.
- The **treasurer's job** either way is to point whoever submits at the Workday walkthroughs on the
  BEARings board and to supply the **budget information to enter plus the group's project number**.

> ⚠️ **Conflict flagged — filing on someone else's behalf.** An earlier version of this file said any
> student-employee member may submit a report for a purchase **someone else** paid for and then repay
> them once the money lands. The current board says only that Workday access requires being an active
> student employee — it does **not** describe filing on another person's behalf. An **archived**
> prior-year card says the opposite: "**ONLY A STUDENT EMPLOYEE can receive a reimbursement**," and tells
> groups with no student-employee member to "contact your business coordinator for an alternative
> solution." Workday pays the person who submits. **Confirm with your Business Coordinator or
> finance@su.wustl.edu before relying on the workaround.**

### Set up Workday email notifications — do this now

Nothing emails you when a report is sent back unless you turn it on. In the board's walkthrough the
**Tasks** notification frequency starts at **Mute**.

1. Click your **profile** → **My Account**.
2. Click **Change Preferences**.
3. Scroll to the **Business Processes** section.
4. Click the three-line button next to the **Tasks** notification type.
5. Set **Frequency** to **Immediately**.
6. Click **OK**, then **Done**.

The walkthrough PDF ("How to set up Workday Inbox Notifications") is attached to the **PROCESS: Spending
from your … Account (In-Depth)** and **REIMBURSEMENTS … (In-Depth)** cards, and stands alone in the
**COMMONLY USED ITEMS** list.

### Before you start: the board's walkthroughs

The **REIMBURSEMENTS: Rules and Processes in one convenient place (In-Depth)** card gathers the rules and
the process in one place, and carries **four attachments**: the proper-receipt example plus three Workday
walkthroughs (submitting a report, checking notes and status, setting up notifications). A one-page
**REIMBURSEMENTS: Quick Reference Guide** summarizes it.

Official WashU instructions: `workday.wustl.edu/items/create-expense-report/` → **"Create an Expense Report
for Yourself (as a worker)"**. In Workday, search **Create Expense Report**.

### Filling out the report — header

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Expense Report For** | Yourself (the student employee submitting) |
| **Creation Options** | **Create New Expense Report**, or copy a previous one |
| **Memo** (header) | The **Event/Budget name** from your operating budget sheet, then the **date of the event** and the **city and state** of the event. Not event-related (overhead, equipment)? Use the **purchase date** and **Saint Louis, MO**. Paying from **Fundraising or Gift**? Use the **WUGO event name** in place of the Event/Budget name |
| **Expense Report Date** | **The date you are entering the report** — not the purchase date |
| **Business Purpose** | **group travel** or **non-travel** |
| **Project** | Your group's **Operating, Fundraising, or Gift project number** — Operating for a budget line or flat funding, Fundraising/Gift when spending those funds. The field is searchable: type your group name and pick the right project |
| **Company / Cost Center / Region** | Normally prefilled. Verify your cost center against `FY27_Project_Numbers_and_Cost_Centers.xlsx` on the board |

### Filling out the report — each expense line

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Expense Date** | Must **match the date on the receipt** |
| **Expense Item** | Always **"Social and Entertainment - Purchases and Supplies"** — this routes the paperwork correctly. Your Business Coordinator re-categorizes it afterward |
| **Quantity / Per Unit / Total Amount** | The total must **match the receipt total** |
| **Memo** (line) | The **Line Item** from your budget line on the Daily Report, then `" - "` and a description of what was purchased. Using Fundraising or Gift funds? Use the **WUGO event name** in place of the Line Item |
| **Attendee(s) / Number of Persons** | Event-related purchase? Enter the **approximate number of people who attended**. **If fewer than 13 attended, every attendee must be named** |
| **Attachments** | The itemized receipt and proof of payment. For event expenses, attach the **approved WUGO event form**. The public event listing does not show formal approval. |
| **Comment** | The **business purpose**: how the purchase serves the event or the group. Accounting needs that context to approve |
| **Receipt Included** | Checked |

For built-in itemizations, use the receipt quantities and unit prices. Include taxes, discounts, and shipping once. Verify zero remaining to itemize, check the project, worktags, and attendance on the child entries where Workday stores them, then reopen the saved report to confirm the values persisted. After submitting, check the live report status and approval routing.

**Report-level limits:**

- **One event per expense report**, maximum **25 lines**. More than that, start a second report.
- **Each unique receipt gets its own line.** Don't bundle receipts onto one line.
- **Upload PDF, JPEG, or PNG only. HEIC files cannot be opened** by the accounting system — a common
  problem with iPhone photos, so convert before uploading.
- **Keep the physical receipt** until the report reads as **fully processed** — you'll want it if an upload
  is blurry.

### Checking status and finding the comments

- **By report number:** search the `EX…` number → open the report → **Business Process** tab → comments are
  in the **rightmost column**. Send-back notes are prefixed **"Send Back Reason:"**.
- **By inbox:** open your **Workday Inbox** → click the report → scroll to the bottom for **View Comments**
  and **Process History**.
- The **Expenses Hub** is the dashboard: create reports, view pending items, track past history.

### Special case: prepaid gas

Upload **both** the original gas receipt **and** a copy/screenshot of the **finalized bank or card
transaction**, so the team can verify the real amount charged rather than the temporary gas-station hold.

### What will get you rejected

- **A bad receipt is the single most common cause of reimbursement delay.** See receipt requirements below.
- Expenses incurred **before funding was available** — never reimbursed, no exceptions.
- **Services paid out of pocket** — DJs, photographers, graphic designers, guest speakers must be paid
  **directly by the university** via WashU credit card, ACH, or university check. Paying out of pocket can
  make you **personally liable**, and the accounting system cannot reimburse personal payments for
  services. See [restrictions.md](restrictions.md).
- **Rental cars paid personally** — must go on a WashU credit card. See [travel.md](travel.md).
- **Monthly bank/credit card statements or canceled checks by themselves** are not acceptable proof of
  purchase.

## 3. Supplier Payment — check or ACH

For a supplier providing goods/services that **cannot accept the WashU credit card**.

**Order matters here:**

1. **Get the contract handled first, with your Campus Life Advisor.** Campus Life owns the *Contracts,
   Agreements and Legal Documents Policy for Student Groups* — route anything signable through your
   advisor rather than signing it yourself. A **WU Letter of Intent** sample is attached to the board's
   spending and COMMONLY USED ITEMS cards. See [campus-life.md](campus-life.md).
2. **Request a formal invoice** from the supplier, and send them the **current W-9 and ACH/EFT forms**
   (linked and attached on the board).
3. **Check the signatures.** Any required signature on the W-9 or ACH form must be **actually signed** —
   **typed signatures are not accepted.** This is a routine rejection cause.
4. Once you have **all** documents, **submit the FTF within 7 days** of whichever comes first: the service
   being completed, the items being received, or **the invoice arriving in your inbox**.
5. **You don't need to wait for a formal approval email.** Your Business Coordinator processes the payment
   and reaches out only if something's wrong.

**Attachments required:** invoice, W-9, ACH electronic payment form, plus anything else that helps.

**The invoice must show all five, to pass university audit:**

| # | Required on the invoice |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Supplier details** — the vendor's official name and business address |
| 2 | **Tracking info** — a unique invoice number and the document date |
| 3 | **Payment terms** — the due date or terms (e.g. "Due upon receipt") |
| 4 | **Your info** — your student group name and the event name |
| 5 | **Itemized breakdown** — exactly what is being paid for, plus the final total |

An **Invoice Template** is attached to the board if your supplier needs one.

> ⚠️ **Two different FTF links on the board.** The **FORMS** card and the RULES cards give one
> Financial Transaction Form for all five actions (`forms.gle/gdFf8bBmXWKJ8nmd6`), but the
> **Non-Credit Card Payments** checklists on all three spending cards link a *different* form
> (`forms.gle/PqKykXFXBh15Skrw7`). Both are live on the board simultaneously and it isn't clear which is
> current for supplier payments. **Ask your Business Coordinator which link to use**, or confirm with
> finance@su.wustl.edu.

**You may not contract WashU employees or student employees for services** — not as a DJ, a staff
photographer, or a faculty speaker. University policy says they can be compensated only through
**Payroll**, and student group accounts cannot process payroll or hourly wages.

## Receipt requirements — every receipt, every method

A valid receipt must show **all** of:

| Requirement | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Store / supplier name** | Where it was purchased from |
| **Date of purchase** | On a reimbursement line, this must match the Expense Date you enter |
| **Items purchased — itemized** | Exactly *what* you bought, not just a total. For restaurants or caterers, the **food and drink items** must be itemized |
| **Prices and the total** | The Workday process checklist asks for **the price of each item** as well as the total |
| **Proof it was actually paid** | A line showing the **last 4 digits of the card used**, or a **$0.00 balance due** / "Paid" |

A card slip with a total and no itemization is not a valid receipt. Neither is an order confirmation, and
neither is a monthly statement or canceled check on its own — those routinely omit the itemization.

**Upload the *entire* receipt**, not a crop or a partial screenshot.

**Paid cash?** If the receipt doesn't say so, write **"paid cash"** in the description field of the request
so the proof-of-payment requirement is met.

**If the itemized receipt lacks payment proof**, ask the vendor for a receipt showing the payment method. If your coordinator accepts a bank or card statement entry as supplemental proof, attach it alongside the itemized receipt. Keep the merchant, date, and settled amount visible and redact account or card numbers and unrelated transactions. If a reviewer cites a different amount, check it against the Workday line, receipt, and actual charge before changing the claim. Keep receipts and statements out of GitHub.

**Best practice:** photograph the physical receipt or download the official PDF invoice **immediately**
after purchase. Reconstructing one later is how the 7-day window gets missed.

**If a receipt is missing details, hold off uploading it** until you have the required info — while keeping
the 7-day clock in mind. Submitting a bad receipt costs more days than fixing it does.

> 📄 **Two model files on the board.** "Example of a Proper Receipt" (`Receipt.pdf`) annotates a real
> receipt with *where / what / when / proof of payment*. "Example of a Credit Card Statement"
> (`Example.pdf`) shows what a statement does **not** prove. Both are in the **COMMONLY USED ITEMS** list.
> Note one inconsistency: the receipt example says **only PDF and JPEG** may be uploaded to the request
> form, while the Workday checklists allow **PDF, JPEG, or PNG**. If PNG is your only option, ask your
> coordinator first.

## Hard spending rules

- **You pay sales tax. Budget for it.** WashU is exempt from Missouri sales tax, but **student groups do not
  get to use the university's exemption** — Financial Services states it "may not be used for personal
  purchases or for those of stand-alone organizations, **including affiliates of the university and student
  organizations**." Paying on a WashU card from a university account does **not** change this. So every
  vendor quote needs tax added before you check it against a budget line, and a verbal quote that sounds
  round ("$3,000") is very often pre-tax. St. Louis city is **9.679%**; rates differ by address, so two
  caterers on the same event can carry different rates. Watch for a **card surcharge** on top (~2.5%) — that
  one *is* avoidable by paying via check or ACH.
- **Spending before funding:** SU will not reimburse students or groups for expenses incurred **before
  funding is available** to the organization. **No exceptions.**
- **Approval strictly precedes spending.** Appeals and reallocations must be **fully approved** before a
  dollar moves. Buy early — or let a vendor contract be signed early — and the new funds cannot cover it;
  **your group, or you personally, eats the bill.** See [appeals.md](appeals.md).
- **Students must run their own transactions.** All transactions on your Operating, Fundraising, or Gift
  accounts must be **initiated and managed by student leaders**. University advisors, academic departments,
  and campus partners **cannot make purchases on your behalf** or process transactions out of your
  accounts — and working with one doesn't exempt you from any SU rule, timeline, or procedure.
- **Use university accounts for everything:** all expenditures must be paid from approved SU Operating,
  Fundraising, or Gift accounts. Group funds must **never** be held in personal accounts or apps (Venmo,
  PayPal, CashApp, etc.), and **no outside bank accounts** for the organization.
- **No account may go negative.** Operating, Fundraising, and Gift all have to stay at or above $0 — see
  [accounts.md](accounts.md).
- **Restricted purchases** can mean denial of purchase, denial of payment, denial of reimbursement, and
  **possible disciplinary action** — see [restrictions.md](restrictions.md). Misuse of funds, fraud, or
  intentional mismanagement is referred straight to **Campus Life and University Administration**.
- **Falling out of compliance pauses everything** — card use, reimbursement processing, supplier payments,
  deposit processing, and reallocations — until the issue is resolved.
- **Watch for spending blackout periods** and the **monthly "Sent Back" fix-by dates**, both posted on the
  Important Financial Dates card. Check near semester start and semester end. See [calendar.md](calendar.md).
