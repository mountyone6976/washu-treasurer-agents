# How WashU student org finance works — the map

Read this first. Everything else in `reference/` is a detail hanging off this structure.

## Two separate authorities, and you need both

Treasurers routinely get blocked because they satisfied one and not the other.

| | **Campus Life** | **SU Business Operations / Treasury** |
|---|---|---|
| Controls | Whether the event may happen | Whether SU money may pay for it |
| Gate | Event registered in **WUGO ≥14 days** before the event | Funding allocated + correct spending process followed |
| Covers | Risk management, food safety, space reservations, alcohol, guest speaker clearance, good standing | Allocations, caps, restricted purchases, payment mechanics |
| Who to ask | **Your Campus Life Advisor** | Your **Business Coordinator** / VP of Finance / Speaker of the Treasury |

A funded event with no WUGO registration doesn't happen. An approved event with no allocation doesn't get
paid for. Track both.

## The money flow

```
                    ┌─ Annual Budget Allocation (spring, for next FY, Cat I only)
  MONEY IN  ────────┼─ Flat Funding (automatic, Cat I & II)          → deposited automatically
                    ├─ Rolling Appeals (as-needed, Cat I only)          into your OPERATING account
                    └─ Fundraising / Gifts (you raise it)            → FUNDRAISING or GIFT account

  YOU HOLD  ────────  three accounts: OPERATING · FUNDRAISING · GIFT   (see accounts.md)

                    ┌─ WashU Credit Card    (in person, DUC 270, Thu/Fri only)
  MONEY OUT ────────┼─ Personal Reimbursement (Workday Expense Report)
                    └─ Supplier Payment      (invoice paid by check or ACH)

  GATED BY  ────────  the Financial Transaction Form (FTF) for most of the above (see ftf.md)
```

You never move money yourself. Awarded funds land in your Operating account automatically; you request
that Business Operations spend it.

## The three questions before any purchase

1. **Is it allowed at all?** → `restrictions.md` + `financial-policies.md`. Restricted purchases and
   over-cap line items get denied at approval/reimbursement time, which is too late.
2. **Which account and which budget line pays for it?** → `accounts.md`. Every FTF asks this. Operating
   (budget/flat funding), Fundraising, or Gift.
3. **Which spending method?** → `spending-methods.md`, then file the FTF per `ftf.md`.

## Non-negotiables that catch treasurers out

- **Spending before funding is never reimbursed. No exceptions.** SU will not cover expenses incurred
  before funding is available to the organization. Get the money allocated *first*.
- **Deficit spending is not funded.** SU will not fund anything you have already spent or committed to.
- **You may now spend before WUGO approval — at your own risk.** Business Ops no longer requires proof of
  registration. But if the event is later **denied**, your group **must repay those funds**, and **may not
  make any additional purchases until it does. No exceptions.**
- **Operating is use-it-or-lose-it.** It resets to zero at fiscal year end. Fundraising and Gift carry over.
- **Event allocations are locked to that event.** They close out once the event's expenses process.
- **Appeal lead time changes your caps.** Travel appeals filed under 4 weeks out get materially lower
  caps than 4+ weeks out. Filing early is worth real money.
- **Re-appeals are refused** unless the event was last funded in your annual budget.
- **80% of revenue from an SU-funded event goes back to SU.**
- **Cash, check, or Cashnet only** for money coming in. **No Venmo, PayPal, CashApp, GoFundMe**, no personal
  accounts, no outside organizational bank accounts.
- **Deposit collected money within 7 days after the event.**
- **You cannot contract WashU employees or student employees for services** — group accounts can't process
  payroll or hourly wages.
- **Advisors and departments cannot transact for you.** Student leaders must start and manage every
  transaction out of the group's accounts.

## The three 7-day clocks

Nearly every deadline in this system is 7 days. Miss one and the money is usually gone:

| Trigger | You have 7 days to |
|---|---|
| You paid out of pocket | Submit the **Workday expense report** |
| Supplier goods received / service provided | Submit the **FTF** |
| You collected money at an event | **Deposit** it |

(Plus: **14 days** before an event to register it in WUGO, and **4 weeks** before to get the better travel
appeal caps.)
