Provided to **all** SU-recognized groups (Category I, II, and III) **free of charge**. Every dollar you
don't spend here is a dollar left for something that actually needs funding — and Treasury's fiscal-
responsibility test explicitly asks whether you minimized costs.

## Worth internalizing

- **Ask for plates, napkins, and utensils** instead of buying them. Recurring, easy saving on any event with
  food.
- **Book a pooled classroom** before pricing a premiere space.
- **Rent HMC equipment** before buying gear.
- **If you use a premiere space, price the IT/AV separately** — that's the line that surprises people.
- **Storage exists.** Don't discard reusable equipment at year end because there's nowhere to put it,
  especially since uniforms and equipment are only funded when they last more than one year.
