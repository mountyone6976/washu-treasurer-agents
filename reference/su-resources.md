don't spend here is a dollar left for something that actually needs funding — and Treasury's fiscal-
responsibility test explicitly asks whether you minimized costs.

## HES event services — free, with a real catch

The card's wording matters, so here it is unpacked:

- SU now offers **HES for set-up, tear-down and clean-up** via an **Event Service Request**. The service
  costs money, but **the expense bypasses your group and is absorbed by the SU General Budget.**
- **You pay the overage if the cost exceeds HES's regular rate** — specifically the **Emergency rate (less
  than 72 hours' notice)** or the **Holiday rate**.
- That overage is taken **first from your Flat Funding in the Operating account**, then from **Fundraising
  and/or Gift**.
- **SU's General Budget does not cover HES *Special* Service Request costs at all.**

> **Practical version: request HES more than 72 hours out.** Miss that and a "free" service starts eating
> your flat funding — the most flexible money your group has.

## Olin Studio 1 & 2 (Performing Arts Department) — training required first

Per the board's HEADS UP card for performance groups needing rehearsal space:

- Read the **PAD Space Guidelines** on `pad.washu.edu`.
- **At least one member of each group must attend a 30-minute training** on using the spaces with **Kelly
  Ging** and **Ryadah Heiskell**, and the group must submit PAD's sign-up form confirming which session
  they'll attend and that they've read the guidelines.
- **Space requests can only be approved after that training is complete.**
- **PAD begins accepting requests two weeks after classes begin** each semester — so a first-fortnight
  rehearsal in Olin isn't bookable, plan around it.
- Contact: pad@wustl.edu.

## Worth internalizing

- **Ask for plates, napkins, and utensils** instead of buying them. Recurring, easy saving on any event with
  food.
- **Book a pooled classroom** before pricing a premiere space.
- **Rent HMC equipment** before buying gear.
- **Ask SUPR for photography** before paying a photographer — services can't be paid out of pocket anyway,
  so a free option removes a whole category of paperwork.
- **If you use a premiere space, price the IT/AV separately** — that's the line that surprises people.
- **Give HES more than 72 hours' notice.**
- **Storage exists.** Don't discard reusable equipment at year end because there's nowhere to put it,
  especially since uniforms and equipment are only funded when they last more than one year.
