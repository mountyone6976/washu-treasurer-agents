# Travel

Travel has the most rules, the tightest caps, and the most ways to leave a student personally on the hook.

## Caps — and why lead time pays

> **FY27 numbers**, verified 2026-09-04 against the **Financial Policies** card
> ([trello.com/c/E5bZT3Ex](https://trello.com/c/E5bZT3Ex)). These caps apply to **Category I groups only** —
> they're the only ones who budget and appeal. The FY27 Budget Allocation Manual, written in spring 2026
> *before* Treasury approved these, still shows older figures; see the conflict callout below.

**The Travel Cap: $712 per active travelling member** (set equal to the Student Activities Fee).

| Line item | Appeal filed **< 4 weeks** out | Appeal filed **4+ weeks** out |
|---|---|---|
| Hotels | $150 / room / night | **$300** / room / night |
| Flights | $300 / round trip | **$400** / round trip |
| Sedans | $65 / day | **$75** / day |
| SUVs | $75 / day | **$85** / day |
| Vans | $85 / day | **$95** / day |
| Gas | $0.20 / mile | $0.20 / mile |

Filing a few days earlier can be worth hundreds of dollars — the hotel cap **doubles** at four weeks.
Note this is *separate* from the appeal submission deadline (3 weeks under $1K, 4 weeks at $1K+) — see
[appeals.md](appeals.md).

> **Hotel cap exception:** Treasury may grant one case by case **if the hotel venue is required by the event
> host**. Ask rather than absorbing the overage.

> ⚠️ **Two live FY27 sources disagree.** The Financial Policies card says travel cap **$712**, conferences
> **$3,500**, gas **$0.20/mile**, flights **$300/$400**. The FY27 BAM and its Funding Limits Executive
> Summary say **$682**, **$3,000**, **$0.15/mile**, **$350**. This file follows the card, which is the
> later-approved document. Which one binds a line **already allocated** in the spring 2026 budget is
> unresolved — confirm with finance@su.wustl.edu before relying on either for a budgeted travel line.

## Distance rules

- **Under 500 miles:** funded **as if the group is driving**, regardless of how you actually travel.
- **500+ miles:** **driving is not permitted.**

> **Gone from FY27:** the FY26 rule that made spring 500+ mile trips depend on whether you travelled 500+
> miles in the fall (two spring trips if not, one if so) does **not** appear on the Financial Policies card.
> The FY27 BAM caps *budgeted* 500+ mile trips at two, but nothing on the board states a current
> **appeal-side** limit. Don't assume you're capped, and don't assume you aren't — ask Treasury.

## Conferences and retreats

| | Limit |
|---|---|
| **Annual conference travel cap** | **$3,500** |
| Conference attendees | **No limit** |
| Retreats | **≤ $100/person** |

**Conference** (FY27 definition): an event providing training, resources, or growth opportunities that can
be brought back and applied to improve group operations, programs, leadership development, or member
engagement. **Attendance must primarily benefit the organization and its members as a whole, rather than
the personal or professional development of individual attendees** — write appeals to that standard.

**Retreat** (FY27 definition): a structured event where a group steps away from typical activities to focus
on growth, planning, team development, collaboration, and/or relationship building.

For both: **if the event is determined to be a travel event, the travel cap also applies.**

> **Gone from FY27:** the ≤4-attendee limit on travel conferences, the "Travel (50–500 miles)" /
> "Local Off-Campus" category definitions, and the list of which line items count against the conference cap
> (Lodging + Registration/Membership Fee + Transportation). None of that appears on the current board in any
> card description or checklist. If you need to know what counts against the $3,500, ask — it is no longer
> documented.

## Attendance tracking is now mandatory

**All travel events except local off-campus travel** must have attendance tracked (new in FY27, same card).
Acceptable methods: **SA Tools** or **reported overall ticket sales**. Plan the mechanism *before* the trip —
this also applies to any event with an allocation over $10,000 and any speaker event over $1,000.

## 🚗 Rental cars — credit card only

- **All car rentals must be paid with a WashU credit card.** **Personal reimbursement is not allowed** —
  paying yourself means you are not covered by university insurance.
- Drivers must be **at least 18** to operate a vehicle.
- Drivers must be **at least 21** to rent anything **larger than a full-size car**.
- Booking a rental is a **travel credit card** transaction: file the FTF, then come in during credit card
  hours (Thu/Fri, 10–12 and 2–4, DUC 270).
- **Travel insurance is a prohibited Operating purchase** — don't add it.

## ✈️ Canceled flights — the trap that bills a student

Airlines issue cancellation credits to the **individual passenger**, not the university. So:

1. **The credit stays with that student** for their future personal use.
2. **The cost must come out of Operating.** Operating funds may only cover *completed* group travel, so the
   unused ticket has to be covered by your **Fundraising and/or Gift** accounts instead.
3. **If those accounts can't cover it, the student who holds the credit must reimburse Student Union** for
   the original ticket cost.

Airline tickets are typically non-refundable. **Confirm your attendance list and dates before booking** —
and tell travelling members this rule *before* they commit, so a dropout isn't a surprise bill.

## FTF fields specific to travel

Beyond the usual (account, budget name, flat funding), a travel FTF asks for:

- Travel **city** and **state**
- **Departure** and **return** dates
- **Upload the roster for this trip**
- **Names and titles** of any **coach, graduate students, or individuals not on your WUGO roster** who are
  travelling
- **Type of purchase**: airfare, hotels, transportation, registration
- The **company/organization** for each purchase
- **Confirmed counts**: rooms, flights, rental cars, registrations

Full field list in [ftf.md](ftf.md).

## Also required

- **Register the trip in WUGO** ≥14 days out, like any event, and follow the Campus Life **Off-Campus Travel
  Policy**.
- **Report incidents** during registered group travel using the Campus Life **Travel Incident Report Form**
  (wustl.presence.io/form/campus-life-incident-report).
- Keep the roster current — travel funding is per **active travelling member**.

## Prepaid gas reimbursement

If someone prepays for gas, the reimbursement needs **both** the gas receipt **and** a copy/screenshot of
the **finalized** bank or card transaction — gas stations place temporary holds, so the receipt alone won't
show the true final amount.
