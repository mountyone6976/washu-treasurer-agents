# Travel

Travel has the most rules, the tightest caps, and the most ways to leave a student personally on the hook.

## Caps — and why lead time pays

**The Travel Cap: $682 per active travelling member** (set equal to the Student Activities Fee).

| Line item | Appeal filed **< 4 weeks** out | Appeal filed **4+ weeks** out |
|---|---|---|
| Hotels | $150 / room / night | **$200** / room / night |
| Flights | $300 / round trip | **$400** / round trip |
| Sedans | $60 / day | **$70** / day |
| SUVs | $70 / day | **$80** / day |
| Vans | $80 / day | **$90** / day |
| Gas | $0.20 / mile | $0.20 / mile |

Filing a few days earlier can be worth hundreds of dollars. Note this is *separate* from the appeal
submission deadline (3 weeks under $1K, 4 weeks at $1K+) — see [appeals.md](appeals.md).

## Distance rules

- **Under 500 miles:** funded **as if the group is driving**, regardless of how you actually travel.
- **500+ miles:** **driving is not permitted.**
- **500+ mile trips in spring** depend on the fall:
  - Didn't travel 500+ miles in the fall → eligible for **two** such trips in spring.
  - Did travel 500+ miles in the fall → **one** appeal for 500+ mile travel in spring.

## Conferences and retreats

| | Limit |
|---|---|
| Travel conference attendees | **≤ 4** |
| Local conference attendees | No restriction |
| **Annual group travel spend** | **$3,000** |
| Retreats | **≤ $100/person** — and the travel cap still stacks on top |

**Travel conference** = event category "Travel (50–500 miles)" or "Travel (500+ miles)".
**Local conference** = "Local Off-Campus".
Counting against the conference travel cap: **Lodging**, **Registration/Membership Fee**, **Transportation**.

## 🚗 Rental cars — credit card only

- **All car rentals must be paid with a WashU credit card.** **Personal reimbursement is not allowed** —
  paying yourself means you are not covered by university insurance.
- Drivers must be **at least 18** to operate a vehicle.
- Drivers must be **at least 21** to rent anything **larger than a full-size car**.
- Booking a rental is a **travel credit card** transaction: file the FTF, then come in during credit card
  hours (Thu/Fri, 10–12 and 2–4, DUC 270).
- **Travel insurance is a prohibited Operating purchase** — don't add it.

## ✈️ Canceled flights — the trap that bills a student

Airlines issue cancellation credits to the **individual passenger**, not the university. So:

1. **The credit stays with that student** for their future personal use.
2. **The cost must come out of Operating.** Operating funds may only cover *completed* group travel, so the
   unused ticket has to be covered by your **Fundraising and/or Gift** accounts instead.
3. **If those accounts can't cover it, the student who holds the credit must reimburse Student Union** for
   the original ticket cost.

Airline tickets are typically non-refundable. **Confirm your attendance list and dates before booking** —
and tell travelling members this rule *before* they commit, so a dropout isn't a surprise bill.

## FTF fields specific to travel

Beyond the usual (account, budget name, flat funding), a travel FTF asks for:

- Travel **city** and **state**
- **Departure** and **return** dates
- **Upload the roster for this trip**
- **Names and titles** of any **coach, graduate students, or individuals not on your WUGO roster** who are
  travelling
- **Type of purchase**: airfare, hotels, transportation, registration
- The **company/organization** for each purchase
- **Confirmed counts**: rooms, flights, rental cars, registrations

Full field list in [ftf.md](ftf.md).

## Also required

- **Register the trip in WUGO** ≥14 days out, like any event, and follow the Campus Life **Off-Campus Travel
  Policy**.
- **Report incidents** during registered group travel using the Campus Life **Travel Incident Report Form**
  (wustl.presence.io/form/campus-life-incident-report).
- Keep the roster current — travel funding is per **active travelling member**.

## Prepaid gas reimbursement

If someone prepays for gas, the reimbursement needs **both** the gas receipt **and** a copy/screenshot of
the **finalized** bank or card transaction — gas stations place temporary holds, so the receipt alone won't
show the true final amount.
