# The treasurer role — duties, expectations, norms

## Your job

You are the **financial executive** for your student organization. Four core duties:

1. **Monitor and manage the organization's budget** using the **Daily Report**.
2. **Spend organizational funds** in accordance with SU, Campus Life, and university policies.
3. **Collect and deposit funds** in accordance with those same policies.
4. Be **actively involved in all** organization financial matters.

## Compliance basics

- Your group's **WUGO roster must be kept updated at all times and must include both a president and a
  treasurer** — and per the Student Group Handbook they **cannot be the same person**.
- A compliant group needs a **minimum of 10 active members** at all times. Full membership rules are in
  [campus-life.md](campus-life.md).
- **Every student group must have an elected treasurer responsible for overseeing all financial matters**
  (Student Group Handbook, "Student Group Finances").
- You are responsible for following the **most current** policies on the SU Finance BEARings Trello board —
  **not** the transition notes you inherited. Predecessor guidance goes stale; the board is authoritative.

### Falling out of compliance pauses your money

The board is explicit about what a compliance pause costs you. **These five services are suspended** until
the issue is resolved:

| Suspended | Meaning |
|---|---|
| WashU credit card usage | No purchasing-card transactions |
| Reimbursement processing | Members don't get paid back |
| Supplier payments | Vendors who don't take cards don't get paid |
| Deposit processing | You can't add funds to your accounts |
| Reallocations | You can't move money between budgets |

Business Ops says they'll reach out to help fix it, and **unresolved issues get referred to Campus Life**
for further review. Separately, **misuse of funds, fraud, or intentional mismanagement is referred
immediately to Campus Life and University Administration.**

## What makes this go well

SU's own framing: understanding the rules and processes, planning ahead, staying organized, and
establishing an internal system that keeps the treasurer informed and involved **throughout event
planning**. Because treasurers oversee allocations, purchases, reimbursements, and collections, your early
involvement is what prevents delays in transactions and appeals.

Translation: the failure mode is an e-board that plans an event and tells the treasurer afterward. Get in
early.

## Communicating with Business Operations

- **Identify yourself and your student group** in every message.
- **Spell out acronyms at least once.** Their example: not everyone knows "PSL" — write "Partners in East
  St. Louis" the first time.
- **Email** for simple questions and routine communication.
- **Credit card hours or a scheduled meeting** for in-depth discussion or guidance.

Business Coordinators handle: spending questions, Workday assistance, deposits, and financial
transactions. The Business Manager (Cathy Winter) handles finance rules, processes, and feedback.

There is also a **Student Group Feedback Form** on the board's FORMS list for feedback to SU and the
Business Operations team — the card says it feeds "minor changes throughout the school year or progressing
operations between school years." And the Treasury team posts reminders on Instagram at **@su_treasury**.

## Boundaries with advisors and campus partners

If your group gets advising from university advisors, academic departments, or other campus partners:

- **You take the lead.** All financial transactions involving your operating, fundraising, or gift accounts
  must be **started and managed directly by student leaders**.
- **Advisors, departments, and campus partners cannot make purchases on your behalf** or process
  transactions out of your accounts for you.
- **One standard for everyone.** Working with an advisor doesn't exempt your group from any SU financial
  rule, timeline, or procedure.

Why it matters: the accounts belong to your group. Staying in the driver's seat keeps you aware of how the
budget is actually being spent and keeps your records accurate in Workday.

## Training requirement — this one gates all spending

### Business Operations Spending Training (BOST), in Canvas

Straight from the board's *Business Operations Spending Training in Canvas* card:

- **Who:** **all presidents and treasurers listed on the student group WUGO roster.**
- **When:** the card says **"before any financial activity can take place"** and "as soon as possible."
  It sets **no calendar deadline** — the deadline is effectively *before your first transaction*.
- **Passing score:** **100%.** "Please be advised that students must receive a 100% score on the course to
  successfully complete the training. If you do not receive a 100%, you will be required to retake the
  course until a score of 100% is achieved." There are knowledge checks throughout as well as the quiz.
- **Where:** Canvas (`wustl.instructure.com`). It is delivered alongside the Campus Life **Student Group
  Leader Training (SGLT)** but scored separately — a group leader can be finished with SGLT and still be
  out of compliance on BOST.

> The current board card sets **no cap on retakes** — it says retake until you hit 100%. If you were told
> "100 attempts," that figure is not on the board; confirm with your Business Coordinator.

> The Canvas course itself requires a WashU login, so an agent can't check your completion status for you.
> The SGLT **online completion window was Jul 31 – Aug 25, 2026** per the Important Financial Dates card
> ([calendar.md](calendar.md)); BOST has no such window.

### The Out of Compliance List — check whether you're on it

The board's **HEADS UP** list carries a card, *Business Operations Spending Training — Out of Compliance
List*, whose only content is a **dated spreadsheet attachment**:
`Groups_that_are_out_of_compliance_-_Business_Operations_Spending_Training_-_MM.DD.YY.xlsx`. The version
live on 2026-09-04 is dated **09.01.26** and lists roughly **80 groups**.

- **What puts you on it:** a president or treasurer on your WUGO roster **has not completed BOST at 100%**.
  That is the only criterion the file tracks — it's a Canvas gradebook export pivoted by organization.
- **What it blocks:** everything in the compliance-pause table above — card use, reimbursements, supplier
  payments, deposits, reallocations. "Before any financial activity can take place" means **your group
  cannot spend while a listed officer is outstanding**, even if *you* personally finished the training.
- **How to check:** `python tools/su_board.py get "Groups_that_are_out_of_compliance" --out /tmp` then
  `python tools/su_board.py xlsx <file>`. Look for your group's name in the visible pivot sheet.
- **Fix:** the named officer completes the Canvas course to 100%. The list is re-exported periodically, so
  re-check the card's date rather than assuming yesterday's file is current.

> **Don't copy this spreadsheet into the repo.** It contains student names, WashU student numbers, Canvas
> IDs and personal email addresses for every officer. Read it from the board when you need it; keep names
> out of `orgs/` and out of `reference/`.

### In-person training with your Business Coordinators

A separate HEADS UP card announces an in-person session: **"We will also be offering an in-person training
session, which is strongly encouraged Aug 31 – Sept 2 in the DUC."** The Important Financial Dates card
lists the same window as **"Business Coordinators Student Group Training (In-Person) Aug 31 – Sept 2."**

- It is **strongly encouraged, not stated as required** — the Canvas course is the one that gates spending.
- **This window ends Sept 2, 2026.** As of the 2026-09-04 board check it is closing today, so treat it as
  effectively closed; the card gives no make-up date. Ask your Business Coordinator for a one-on-one
  instead — they take bookings (see [contacts-su.md](contacts-su.md)).

SU covers funding in more depth in its own separate training, and the Spring 2027 dates include an
undated **"SU Finance BEARings Training — Spring Semester."**
