# WUGO SU Financial Appeals: fields and submission workflow

Observed in the live [SU Financial Appeals form](https://wustl.presence.io/form/su-financial-appeals) on
2026-09-28. This is a form walkthrough, not a new funding policy. Recheck the live form when it differs.
See [appeals.md](appeals.md) for eligibility, deadlines, and funding rules.

## Prepare once, ask once

1. Resolve the active organization from `orgs/ACTIVE_ORG`. Read its profile, contacts, current allocation,
   prior appeals, and any event record. Reuse confirmed answers from the conversation.
2. Read the organization's WUGO profile and constitution when mission, category, or membership definition
   is missing. A roster's total membership is not the active undergraduate count. Record the source and
   date in the private org profile so the next appeal does not repeat the research.
3. Inspect the actual form and relevant conditional sections early. Build a missing-facts list, then ask
   one concise batch of questions instead of discovering a new requirement after each reply.
4. Distinguish required form facts from optional supporting detail. Ask only for missing facts that affect
   the request. Use N/A where applicable and allowed; never invent a dollar amount or use zero for an
   unknown. Attendance may be an explicitly identified planning estimate.
5. Resolve dates to a calendar date and weekday in the event's timezone. If relative expressions conflict,
   state the interpretation and resolve material ambiguity before filing. Record event start/end and time
   separately from the date funds must be spent.
6. Check whether the requested expense is already purchased or committed, whether this is a new request
   or supplements an annual budget, and whether the event previously received appeal funding.
7. Check normal lead time once. If the treasurer reports that a late submission has been cleared and
   explicitly directs filing, record this as treasurer-reported clearance and proceed with the authorized
   submission. Do not turn that into a general policy exception, claim approval, or repeatedly ask for the
   same permission. Already-spent/committed funding eligibility is a separate issue.

## Core required fields

An asterisk below reflects a required field observed on the form.

| Form label | What to prepare |
|---|---|
| Student Group Name * | Select the registered organization. The form warns that a submitter who is not the group's treasurer will be automatically denied. |
| Event/Request Title * | Consistent event or request name. |
| Event Category * | Select from live options; On-Campus was observed. |
| Please provide a detailed description of the request, including how it relates to your group's mission. * | What, when, where, co-hosts, purpose, mission connection, exact expense and why it is needed. |
| Please state your group's mission * | Constitution-based mission; do not invent a mission from the event theme. |
| How many active undergraduate members are in your group? * | Current active undergraduate count, source, and confirmation date. |
| How does your group define an "active member"? * | Actual organization definition, preferably from its constitution; separate from the count. |

## Conditional sections: avoid irrelevant questions without hiding facts

### Funding source and co-hosts

Collaboration and outside funding are separate questions. A co-host paying part of the event from its
existing SU allocation is not automatically an outside funding source. When the treasurer confirms all
event funding originates with SU, select **No - Student Union is our only source of funding for this event**
and explain the co-host's SU-funded contribution in the alternatives narrative. Check for duplicate
requests or use of an allocation outside its approved purpose; this classification is not spending approval.

If funding actually comes from a source other than SU, select **Yes - we are receiving funding from more
than just Student Union**. This revealed two required fields:

- **Individual / Organization Providing Additional Funding**
- **Amount of Money Received / To Be Received**

Do not guess the contribution, enter zero to bypass validation, or relabel outside money as SU money.
If genuinely required facts are missing, save the draft and ask for them together.

### Expense scope

Observed choices: Airbnb, Hotels, Cars, Ubers, Flights, Buses, Food, Venue Rental,
Supplies (for an activity), Speaker/Guest, Registration Fees/Tickets, and Other.

The label says "used for this event," so do not silently assume that it always means requested expenses
only. For a narrowly scoped appeal, follow the treasurer's explicit scope clarification: select the
categories relevant to this appeal and disclose separately funded costs in the narrative. For example,
a film-license-only request may use **Other** while co-hosts cover refreshments separately. Selecting Food
unnecessarily creates required menu/vendor/service questions. If the current form explicitly requires
all event categories, follow that instruction and answer the relevant sections truthfully.

Selecting **Food** revealed these required questions:

- What food will be at the event?
- What is the food being used for?
- Where will you be obtaining the food from? Have you considered other places?
- Is food required for the execution of the event? Please explain.
- Do you require servers for the event? Please explain.

Selecting **Other** revealed:

- Please explain anything that does not fit into the aforementioned categories - What is it, why is it
  required for this event, and how you arrived at the final cost.

Other expense branches and paid-admission follow-ups were not inspected in this walkthrough. Read them
live rather than treating this document as an exhaustive schema. After changing a controlling answer,
wait for conditional sections to update and verify which required fields remain.

## Financial questions and evidence

Line items are repeatable with **Add** and should be entered in priority order, most crucial first.

| Form label | What to prepare |
|---|---|
| Line Item Description * | Specific requested item/service. |
| Line Item Category * | Choose from live options; Miscellaneous was available for an uncategorized license fee. Do not assume it fits every request. |
| Requested Amount ($) * | Amount for this line. |
| How did you arrive at this number for this line item? * | Quantity × unit price, and any relevant cap calculation. |
| What is the total amount of funding (in $) that you are requesting (sum of Line Items above)? * | Sum of requested lines, not the entire event budget when others cover separate costs. |
| Briefly describe any measures you took to minimize costs for this request. * | Concrete measures actually taken; do not invent quotes or comparisons. |
| Please upload all relevant documentation for each line item | Observed upload had no required asterisk. Examples included quotes, previous invoices, travel rosters, and item listings with price. Check the live form and policy for the request. |

Describe evidence accurately. An event-review comment requiring a license supports necessity, not the
quoted license price or completed approval. A treasurer-supplied cost is not a vendor quote. Record any
missing quote without fabricating one or turning an optional attachment into a mandatory blocker.

## Browser workflow that worked

Use the browser tools available in the current environment and obey their tool documentation. The
following are interaction lessons, not a script with reusable session identifiers.

1. Open WUGO and try its normal **Login** flow using the available authenticated session. Do not assume a
   remote user can see or operate your tab. Ask for user intervention only if credentials, MFA, or another
   actual login blocker remains. Never store credentials or session data in the repository.
2. Open the correct form or resume the saved response; do not create a duplicate appeal after a timeout.
   Confirm the submitting account and organization. Use fresh visible labels/roles, not remembered field
   IDs or a previous browser session's tab IDs.
3. Select dropdown options through the visible custom dropdown. A native select's value can change before
   the displayed choice and dependent questions update. Verify both before continuing.
4. Replace whole textbox contents rather than appending large batches of typed text. Blur the final edited
   field and verify the new value before Save or Submit; immediate submission can race an app update.
5. Date inputs need special care: a plain fill followed by Tab reverted to today's date in the observed
   widget. Focusing the date input, selecting all, typing MM/dd/yyyy and pressing Enter worked. Set both
   dates, inspect their displayed values, and verify them again in the saved response.
6. To pause, use the split-button menu beside **Submit for Approval** → **Save as Draft**. Preserve the
   response URL and mark the tab for handoff if the browser tool supports it. Saving may navigate
   asynchronously; wait for the saved view before making further edits.
7. When the user has explicitly requested submission and the form is complete, click **Submit for Approval**.
   A request to fill or draft alone is not authorization to submit. Existing explicit authorization remains
   valid; do not ask again just because an old repository instruction says "draft only." Submission is not
   authorization to buy, sign a contract, or send a separate email.
8. If validation fails, collect all reported errors. Resolve known facts immediately; ask only for facts
   still genuinely missing after checking the correct conditional branches. Do not repeatedly click Submit
   without a relevant correction.
9. After submission, allow the response view to finish loading; refresh once if it is showing stale data.
   Verify persisted fields and an actual submission/approval workflow signal. The generic **Submitted By**
   label appears on saved drafts too and does not prove submission. In the observed successful flow,
   refreshing showed an **Approvals** tab and assigned reviewer workflow.
10. An assigned reviewer or a label ending in "Approves" is not an approval decision. Report **submitted,
    awaiting review** until an explicit decision appears. Save the response link, timestamp, status evidence,
    and submitted answers in the private appeal record. Capture screenshot proof where supported, keeping
    it out of the public template and respecting the repository's source-document ignore rules.

## Reuse and privacy

Copy [the blank appeal record](../orgs/_template/appeals/appeal-record.md) into the active organization's
appeals directory, then fill it with the actual request and submitted answers. Keep stable facts such as
mission and active-member definition in the org profile, with sources and dates. Attendance, free admission,
co-host funding, and timing clearance are event-specific and must not become future defaults.

Shared agents, this guide, and the public starter record must contain no real group profile, membership
figure, event details, personal contacts, response IDs, private URLs, screenshots, or account identifiers.
Transfer general workflow lessons only; never copy a private repository wholesale into the public template.
